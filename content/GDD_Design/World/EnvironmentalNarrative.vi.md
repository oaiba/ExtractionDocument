## Environmental Storytelling Philosophy

**Principle:** The world tells the story itself - no need for lengthy cutscenes.

**Methods:**
- Visual clues (traces, items, graffiti)
- Audio logs (short recordings 30-60s)
- Environmental contrasts
- Implied history thông qua decay

## Industrial Decay - Regional Stories

### Reactor Tower - "The Meltdown"

**Lore Background:**
Year 2029, the Power Plant was operating normally. khi The Collapse occurred, the cooling hệ thống was hacked. Workers attempted a manual shutdown, nhưng the radiation leak had already spread.

**Environmental Traces:**

| Location | Trace | Implication |
|:-------|:--------|:--------|
| Floor 1 | Worker corpses in protective suits | Attempted manual shutdown |
| Floor 2 | Barricade made of equipment | Workers trapped above |
| Floor 3 | Control panels destroyed | Sabotage hoặc panic |
| Basement | Bloodtrail leading to tunnel | Survivors escaped |

**Audio Log #1: "Final Shift"**
> *"This is Foreman Lee... Day 3 sau the lockdown. The radiation is rising. We sealed the lower levels nhưng... [static] ...Martinez didn't make it. nếu anyone finds this, the emergency tunnel leads to the workshops. Don't trust the—" [cut off]*

**Visual chi tiết:**
- Clock stopped at 3:47 AM
- Wall calendar: March 15, 2031
- Emergency lights still flickering (separate generator)
- Radiation cảnh báo signs freshly painted in red

## Bloodstain Storytelling Guide

### Bloodstain Types & Meanings

| Type | Visual | Story Implication |
|:-----|:-------|:------------------|
| **Splatter** | Radial pattern | Violence, gunfire |
| **Pool** | Large static puddle | Body rested here |
| **Drag Mark** | Linear streak | Body moved |
| **Handprint** | Palm on wall | Wounded person walking |
| **Footprints** | Bloody shoe marks | Walker thông qua blood |

### Placement Guidelines

**DO:**
- Lead to loot locations (reward exploration)
- tạo implied narratives
- Vary from old (brown) to recent (red)
- Connect to audio logs

**DON'T:**
- Excessive gore mà không mục đích
- Random placement
- Near spawn areas (immersion break)
- Child-related violence

## Environmental Puzzle Hints

### Hidden Loot Clues

**Pattern:** Environmental chi tiết hint at nearby secrets.

**Examples:**

| Clue | Location | Secret |
|:-----|:---------|:-------|
| Arrow graffiti | Warehouse W5 | Hidden floor cache |
| Scratched wall thông số | Office 3F | Safe code nearby |
| Candles lit in circle | Lab basement | Special item spawn |
| Fresh bloodtrail | Any | Recent PvP happened here |
