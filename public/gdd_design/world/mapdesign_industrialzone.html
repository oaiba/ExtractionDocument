    Map Design: Industrial Zone (The “Factory”)

    ← Back to Index | Next: Art Direction →

    🗺️ Map Overview

    • Name: Sector 7 Industrial Complex (aka “The Factory”)
    • Theme: Abandoned Soviet-era processing plant repurposed for anomalous research.
    • Size: Medium (1200m x 1200m)
    • Player Count: 12 Players (Solo/Duo/Squads) + 20 AI Bots
    • Raid Time: 20 Minutes
    • Difficulty: Intermediate (Balanced Loot/Risk)

    Narrative Context: Once the primary refining facility for local crystalline resources. When the “Outbreak” occurred, the military sealed the exits, trapping workers inside. Now, it’s a scavenger’s paradise filled with raw materials, left-behind military gear, and dangerous chemical leaks.

    📍 Tactical Layout

    The map is a “Bowl” design: High ground on the perimeter (Forest/Hills), low ground in the center (Factory Complex), promoting inward movement.

           [Forest/Hills] (High Ground)
      /      |       \
     /   [Train Yard] \
    |         |        |
[Storage]--[BRIDGE]--[OFFICES]
    |         |        |
    |      [FACTORY]---|
    |      (Low/Hot)   |
[Parking]----|----[Main Gate]
    |        |         |
    \    [Perimeter]   /
     \_______|________/

    Zone 1: The Perimeter (Green Zone)

    Safe start, low loot, long sightlines.

    • 1A. Train Yard (North): Rusted cargo trains. Good linear cover. Sniper vantage points on train cars.
    • 1B. Storage Units (West): Rows of garage-style storage. Close-quarters combat (CQC). “Rat” looting spots.
    • 1C. Parking Lot (South): Abandoned civilian cars. Open area, hazardous to cross.

    Zone 2: The Complex (Yellow Zone)

    Moderate risk, industrial combat, verticality.

    • 2A. Assembly Hall: Huge open interior with conveyor belts. 2 levels (Ground + Catwalks). High verticality.
    • 2B. Offices: 3-story brick building attached to the hall. Tight corridors, breaching doors, filing cabinets (Intel loot).
    • 2C. Cooling Towers: Two massive towers. External spiral stairs leading to high ground sniping spots.

    Zone 3: The Lab (Red Zone / Hotspot)

    Extreme risk, highest tier loot, Boss spawn.

    • Location: Underground, accessible via 2A Assembly Hall elevator shaft or 2B Office basement.
    • Layout: Claustrophobic white corridors, broken glass, flickering lights.
    • Threats: High-level AI guards, The “Warden” (Boss).
    • Loot: Keycards, Injectors, High-tech weapon parts.

    🛠️ Operator Tactical Interactions

    Each class can exploit the map’s industrial nature differently:

    Class Interaction Benefit Location Examples
    TANK Heavy Breach Smash through weak brick walls or force open rusted gates. Offices (1F): Create new entry points. Storage: Break metal shutters.
    RECON Signal Hack Hack CCTV cameras to reveal enemies on minimap for 30s. Security Room (offices), Factory Main Gate.
    SPECIALIST Generator Overload Turn on/off industrial lights or electrified water hazards. Cooling Towers: Create noise cover. Canal: Electrify water to kill rushers.
    SUPPORT Cargo Lift Can operate heavy elevators to move squad vertically. Assembly Hall: Move team to catwalks silently.
    • Destructible Environment: Catwalk railings can be shot out to drop enemies; Oil drums explode.

    💎 Loot Distribution & Heatmap

    Area / POI Loot Tier Item Types Danger Level Recommended For
    The Lab (Underground) S-Tier (Highest) Keycards, Boss Gear, Injectors 💀 Extreme (Boss + Players) Squads / Geared Solos
    Assembly Hall A-Tier (High) Weapon Parts, Industrial Tech 🔴 High (Sniper Sightlines) PvP Seekers
    Offices B-Tier (Mid) Intel Items, Meds, Cash 🟠 Medium (CQC Ambush) Solos / Scavengers
    Train Yard C-Tier (Low) Ammo, Basic Components 🟢 Low (Perimeter Spawn) Fresh Spawns / Snipe setup
    Storage Units C-Tier (Low) Food, Construction Materials 🟢 Low (Good Cover) “Rat” Runs
    • Hotspot: The Lab entrance (Assembly Hall) attracts 70% of players eventually.
    • Safe Route: Skirting the perimeter from Storage → Train Yard → Gate 03 avoids most conflict but yields poor loot.

    ⚔️ Choke Points & Flow

    Extraction shooters live and die by their Choke Points - areas where players are forced to interact.

    1. “The Bridge” (Connecting West Storage to Factory)

    • Description: A narrow metal skybridge over a chemical canal.
    • Tactical: The only direct path from West Spawn to the Hotspot without going through the open canal (slow movement).
    • Danger: High. Easy to camp from the Office windows.

    2. “Main Gate” (Factory Entrance)

    • Description: The primary reinforced gate into the compound. Slightly open, creating a funnel.
    • Tactical: High traffic area for vehicles (future) or squads rushing the center.
    • Danger: Variable. AI Scavs often patrol here.

    3. “The Vent” (Lab Entrance)

    • Description: A destructible ventilation grate leading into the Lab.
    • Tactical: Requires explosives or a crowbar to open. Noisy entry.
    • Danger: Extreme. “Dinner bell” for nearby players.

    🎨 Environmental Design & Atmosphere

    Visual Identity

    • Palette: Rust Orange, Concrete Grey, Toxic Green (Chemicals).
    • Lighting: Overcast day (Standard). Interiors are dim, requiring flashlights/NVGs in the Lab.
    • Details:
      • Storytelling: “Help Us” painted on the roof.
      • Assets: Forklifts, pallets of rotting supplies, military checkpoints.

    Audio Landscape

    • Ambient: Distant humming of the generator (directional clue), dripping water in tunnels, crows cawing outside.
    • Surfaces:
      • Metal (Catwalks): Loud, clanky. punishment for sprinting.
      • Glass (Offices): Crunching noise (Crucial for hearing flankers).
      • Puddles (Canal): Splashing sounds.

    🗓️ Evolution Plan (Live Ops)

    • Phase 1 (Launch): Ground floor & 1st floor only. Lab sealed.
    • Phase 2: The “Lab” opens. New Boss added.
    • Phase 3: Dynamic Event - “Chemical Leak” floods the lower tunnels, forcing players to high ground.
