    Map Design: Neon Slums (The “Undercity”)#

    ← Back to Index | Next: Art Direction →

    🗺️ Map Overview#

    • Name: District 09 (aka “Neon Slums”)
    • Theme: Cyberpunk-favela built on the ruins of a mega-city. Dense, vertical, and neon-lit.
    • Size: Small/Dense (800m x 800m)
    • Player Count: 8 Players (Focus on Solo/Duo)
    • Raid Time: 15 Minutes
    • Difficulty: High (CQC focus, relentless verticality)

    Unique Feature: Dynamic Day/Night Cycle. The raid starts at dusk and ends in pitch black night, forcing players to use flashlights or NVGs in the final minutes.

    📍 Tactical Layout#

    The map is a “Favela” design: Layers built on top of layers. rooftops are highways; streets are death traps.

     LEVEL 3: [PENTHOUSE] (Sniper Nest)
              |
              | (Elevator/Scaffolding)
              |
 LEVEL 2: [Apt Block A]--[BRIDGE]--[Apt Block B]--[Arcade]
              |            |            |
              | (Stairs)   | (Jump)     | (Ramp)
              |            v            |
 LEVEL 1: [Market]----[Canal]----[Alleyways]
              |
              | (Stairs)
              v
 LEVEL 0: [Subway Station] (Underground)

    Zone 1: The Gutters (Street Level)#

    Safe spawns, but dangerous to traverse.

    • 1A. The Market: Narrow alleyways cluttered with stalls. High occlusion (easy to hide, hard to see far).
    • 1B. Sewage Canal: Runs through the center. Slow movement speed deep water.
    • 1C. Subway Entrance: Only access to the localized underground metro station (Mid-tier loot).

    Zone 2: The Stacks (Apartments)#

    Room-to-room combat, ambush heaven.

    • 2A. Apartment Block A & B: 4-story buildings connected by makeshift bridges. Destructible walls.
    • 2B. The Arcade: Neon-filled gaming hall. Loud ambient noise (distracts from footsteps). Good energy weapon loot.

    Zone 3: The Penthouse (Hotspot)#

    Highest ground, sniper paradise.

    • Location: Top of the central skyscraper ruin. Accessible only via elevator (noisy) or external scaffolding (exposed).
    • Threats: Elite Sniper AI.
    • Loot: Data Chips, Legendary Sniper Parts.

    🛠️ Operator Tactical Interactions#

    Navigating the vertical favela requires special tools:

    Class Interaction Benefit Location Examples
    RECON Zipline Setup Shoot a temporary zipline for the whole team. Rooftops: Cross between Apartment A and B instantly.
    ASSAULT Parkour Leap Jump further/higher to reach hidden balconies. Alleyways: Reach 2nd floor windows without stairs.
    SPECIALIST Drone Vent Send drones through small AC vents to scout rooms. Penthouse: Check for campers before breaching.
    TANK Shield Wall Deploy portable cover on narrow bridges. Bridge of Sighs: Block sniper fire while crossing.
    • Cyber-Hacks: Hack neon signs to overload/explode them (Blind enemies). Hack Subway gates to lock sections.

    💎 Loot Distribution & Heatmap (Verticality Focus)#

    Floor / Area Loot Tier Item Types Danger Level Recommended Key
    Penthouse (Lvl 3) S-Tier Legendary Sniper Rifles 💀 Extreme (1 Entry point) King of the Hill
    Arcade (Lvl 2) A-Tier Electronics, Energy Cells 🔴 High (Loud Audio) Balanced Squads
    Apartments (Lvl 2) B-Tier Domestic Items, Cash 🟠 Medium (Wallbangs) Campers / CQC
    Market (Lvl 1) C-Tier Food, Trash Loot 🔴 High (Visible from above) Risky Transit
    Subway (Lvl 0) B-Tier Tools, Quest Items 🟠 Medium (Grenade traps) Flank Route
    • Heatmap: Most deaths occur at the “Bridge of Sighs” and the Subway Entrance.
    • The Trap: The Market (Street Level) has low-tier loot but high danger from rooftop snipers. Avoid lingering here.

    ⚔️ Choke Points & Flow#

    1. “Bridge of Sighs” (Connecting Apt A & B)#

    • Description: A rickety wooden bridge connecting the 3rd floors of the two main buildings.
    • Tactical: Fastest way to cross without dropping to street level.
    • Danger: Exposed to snipers from The Penthouse.

    2. “The Metro Turnstile”#

    • Description: Narrow entrance to the subway.
    • Danger: Grenade trap heaven.

    🚪 Extraction Points#

    Subway Train Timed Arrives every 5 mins Underground Station

    Sewer Grate Always Open None Canal Finish Line

    🎨 Environmental Design#

    • Palette: Neon Pink/Blue, Dirty Concrete, Rain-slicked Asphalt.
    • Lighting: High contrast. Dark shadows vs blinding neon signs.
    • Audio: Constant rain (muffles light footsteps), distant sirens, cyberpunk synth music in the Arcade.
