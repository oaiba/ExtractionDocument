# Extraction Shooter Game Design Document

Welcome to the official Game Design Document (GDD) for our Top-Down Mobile Extraction Shooter.

## 📚 Documentation Structure

### [Design & Creative Hub]({{< relref "/docs/GDD_Design" >}})
Focuses on game design, art direction, and player experience.
* **World Building**: Maps, environmental narrative, loot.
* **Gameplay**: Mechanics, combat, progression.
* **Visuals & Audio**: Art direction, UI, sound design.

### [Technical & Engineering Hub]({{< relref "/docs/GDD_Technical" >}})
Focuses on technical implementation, architecture, and systems.
* **Core Systems**: Networking, architecture, roadmap.
* **Gameplay Systems**: Character, weapon, inventory logic.
* **Performance**: Optimization guidelines.
