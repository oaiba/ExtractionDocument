## 🎨 The Aesthetic of Decay

The visual identity of **Extraction Shooter** is grounded, gritty, and atmospheric. We prioritize **readability** over excessive detail, ensuring that gameplay elements stand out against the environment.

### Art Pillars
1.  **Readability:** Lighting and value contrast guide the player's eye.
2.  **Atmosphere:** Dense volumetrics, dynamic weather, and environmental storytelling.
3.  **Optimization:** Assets built for performance without sacrificing fidelity.
