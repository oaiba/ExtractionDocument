## 🎓 Design Goal: "Teach to Survive"

The tutorial is **NOT** a shooting gallery. It is a **Safety Drill**.
Most players quit Extraction Shooters because they loot, get lost, and die to a camper without knowing why.
"Operation Zero" mimics a real raid but removes the punishment (Gear Fear).

### Learning Objectives
1.  **Movement & Noise:** "Slow is Smooth, Smooth is Fast."
2.  **Looting Logic:** Identify containers vs. trash.
3.  **Healing:** Fixing a "Heavy Bleed" vs. "Light Bleed".
4.  **Extraction:** The panic of the timer.

---

## 🗺️ The Mission Script

### Phase 1: The Crash (Movement)
*   **Context:** You wake up near a crashed helicopter in a dense forest (safe start).
*   **Action:**
    *   **Crawl:** Under fallen debris (Force Crouch/Prone).
    *   **Vault:** Over a broken wing.
    *   **Injure:** Scripted fall causes a "Fracture".
    *   **Heal:** Use a splint found on a dead pilot. *Teaches medical menu.*

### 2. The Scavenger Camp (Stealth/Combat)
*   **Context:** You see 2 Scavengers looting a campfire.
*   **Choice:**
    *   **Stealth:** Crouch-walk through tall grass (UI shows "Noise Meter").
    *   **Loud:** Shoot them. (If you miss, they yell, summoning a 3rd reinforcement).
*   **Result:** Loot their bodies. Find a **Keycard** (Quest Item).

### 3. The Bunker (Looting)
*   **Context:** Use the Keycard to open a bunker door.
*   **Action:**
    *   **Search:** Find a weapon box. *Teaches "Search Time".*
    *   **Tetris:** The box contains a large Vest. You must rearrange your inventory to fit it.
    *   **Equip:** Put on the Vest and a Helmet.

### 4. The Extraction (Panic)
*   **Context:** Alarm triggers! "Extraction Zone Active: 60 Seconds".
*   **Action:** Sprint to the flare smoke.
*   **Twist:** An AI Sniper shoots near you (scripted miss) to induce stress. You must zigzag or slide to cover.
*   **Exfil:** Hold "F" at the flare. Wait 10s. Screen fades to black.

## 🧠 "Soft" Tutorials (Loading Screen Tips)

Don't rely just on the raid. Use the downtime.
*   *"Sound is your radar. If you can hear them, they can hear you."*
*   *"Don't peek the same angle twice."*
*   *"Loot is heavy. Stamina drains faster when over 30kg."*
*   *"If you find an Injector, check the description. Some kill you."*

---

## 🚫 Anti-Frustration Features

*   **God Mode (Invisible):** Player HP cannot drop below 1 during tutorial. (They fumble, screen turns red, but they don't die and restart).
*   **Infinite Ammo:** Magazines refill instantly *only* in this mission.
*   **Waypoint Markers:** 3D UI markers guide the path (features disabled in main game).

## 🎁 Reward: "Starter Kit"

Completing the tutorial grants:
1.  **Operator:** "Rookie" (Level 1).
2.  **Weapon:** M4A1 (Standard) + 3 Mags.
3.  **Armor:** Class 2 Vest (Damaged).
4.  **Currency:** $5,000 (Enough for 1 raid insurance).

This gear is *yours*. If you lose it in the next raid, it's gone forever. Welcome to Aethelgard.
