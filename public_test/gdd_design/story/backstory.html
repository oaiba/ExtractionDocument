      World History & Backstory

      Canonical Timeline

      This document is the single source of truth for all historical events in the game world. All other documents reference this timeline.

      Pre-Collapse Era (2020-2030)

      The Age of Instability (2020-2025)

      The world did not collapse overnight. It eroded.

      Year Event Consequence
      2020-2021 COVID-19 pandemic Global supply chains fracture. Remote work normalizes. Public trust in institutions drops by 40%
      2022 Energy crisis (Ukraine conflict escalation) European energy markets collapse. Fuel prices triple. Rationing begins in 12 countries
      2023 “The Great Resignation” reaches critical mass Labor shortages in critical infrastructure (hospitals, power plants, logistics). Automation accelerates
      2024 AI Act failures Regulatory frameworks lag behind AI capabilities. Deepfakes destabilize elections in 6 nations
      2025 “The Water Wars” begin Freshwater scarcity triggers border conflicts in Central Asia, North Africa, and the American Southwest. The UN Security Council deadlocks

      Design Note: These events are recognizable extensions of real-world trends. This grounds the fiction in plausibility — players should feel “this could happen.”

      The Corporate Age (2025-2029)

      As governments weakened, corporations filled the vacuum.

      The Rise of Nexus Corporation

      Year Nexus Corp Milestone Public Perception Reality
      2025 Nexus provides emergency power grid to 3 nations “Corporate savior” Leveraging crisis for infrastructure control
      2026 Nexus launches “SafeNet” — free mesh internet for developing nations “Democratizing access” Mass surveillance network. Data collection on 800M users
      2027 Nexus subsidiary wins UN contract for disaster relief logistics “Essential partner” Embedding corporate agents in government emergency systems
      2028 Nexus acquires 4 pharmaceutical companies, 2 defense contractors “Diversified investment” Creating vertical integration for Project Prometheus
      2029 Nexus revenue exceeds GDP of 40 nations. Net worth: $12 trillion “Too big to fail” Too powerful to stop

      The Neo-Asia Industrial Corridor (NAIC)

      The NAIC was Nexus Corp’s flagship project — a massive manufacturing and technology hub spanning a fictional region inspired by East and Southeast Asian urban sprawl.

      Concept:

      • A Special Economic Zone with zero government oversight — Nexus was the law
      • Population: 3 million workers relocated with promises of employment and housing
      • Infrastructure: Power plants, chemical processing, electronics manufacturing, residential towers, commercial districts
      • Hidden purpose: Provide cover for Project Prometheus research facilities

      Inspirations:

      • Shenzhen’s explosive growth (speed of development)
      • Korean DMZ aesthetic (militarized perimeter)
      • Japanese industrial decay (abandoned factories)
      • Southeast Asian urban density (vertical sprawl, mixed-use chaos)

      The Final Year (2030)

      Quarter Event Warning Signs Ignored
      Q1 Internal Nexus audit reveals “systemic containment risks” Report classified. Auditor reassigned to Antarctica facility
      Q2 Major data leak: “Prometheus Files” appear on darknet Nexus claims forgery. Sends legal teams after journalists. 3 reporters disappear
      Q3 Global protests against corporate overreach. “Break Nexus” movement Nexus funds counter-protests. Social media manipulation via SafeNet
      Q4 Project Prometheus Subject 7 shows “anomalous cognition” Internal panic. Emergency containment protocols activated too late

      The Collapse (2031)

      The Triangle of Disaster

      Three simultaneous crises created a cascading failure that no single institution could contain:

                 CYBERATTACK
           Infrastructure paralyzed
              /          \
             /   PERFECT   \
            /    STORM      \
           /                 \
      PANDEMIC          RESOURCE WAR
      15-20% mortality  Nations fracture

      Detailed Event Chain

      Date Event Immediate Consequence Long-term Impact
      Jan 15 Global Blackout — Coordinated cyberattack on power infrastructure across 47 countries 72-hour total power loss in major cities. Hospitals switch to generators. Internet goes dark Permanent distrust of digital infrastructure. Cash economy resurges briefly
      Jan 20 Banking System Crash — Financial networks data corrupted beyond repair Economic collapse. Savings evaporate. Stock markets never reopen Currency becomes meaningless. Barter economy emerges within weeks
      Feb 1 “Crimson Flu” appears — Novel pathogen in NAIC region Initial containment attempted. WHO declares emergency Origin suspect: Project Prometheus lab leak (never officially confirmed)
      Feb 14 Quarantine fails — Crimson Flu crosses containment perimeter Global spread within 2 weeks. 15-20% mortality rate. Hospitals collapse No healthcare system survives. Medical knowledge becomes the most valuable commodity
      Mar 1 Food riots — Supply chain collapse reaches critical point Supermarkets looted within hours. Farms militarized. Water treatment plants abandoned Starvation becomes the primary killer, surpassing the virus by Month 3
      Mar 15 Military splinters — Chain of command breaks as soldiers prioritize families Desertion rate hits 60%. Remaining units form warlord factions Organized violence replaces organized law. The strong take from the weak
      Apr 1 Governments fall — Last functioning states dissolve No more borders, laws, or emergency services The old world is officially dead
      Apr 15 The Collapse complete — Global civilization as a unified system ceases to exist Isolated pockets of order remain (military bases, corporate fortresses, island communities) The Exclusion Zone era begins

      Who Caused It?

      The truth (revealed gradually across Seasons 1-3):

      Nobody “caused” The Collapse by themselves. But Nexus Corporation was the primary catalyst:

      1. Project Prometheus created enhanced human subjects whose containment failure may have triggered or accelerated the Crimson Flu pandemic
      2. Nexus’s SafeNet infrastructure had backdoors that hackers exploited for the cyberattack — vulnerabilities Nexus knew about but chose not to patch (it would have reduced data collection)
      3. Nexus’s monopolization of critical infrastructure meant no redundancy existed when systems failed

      The uncomfortable truth: The Collapse was preventable. Nexus chose profit over safety at every decision point.

      Project Prometheus (2026-2031)

      Program Overview 

      PROJECT PROMETHEUS — "Enhancement Through Integration"
Classification: NEXUS LEVEL BLACK
Location: NAIC Tech Labs (Labs Alpha, Beta, Gamma)
Director: Dr. Seo-Yun Park (later defected → Tech Syndicate leader)
Lead Researcher: Dr. Viktor Chen (deceased? — conflicting reports)
Budget: $4.7 billion (hidden across 12 subsidiary ledgers)

      Research Phases

      Phase Years Objective Method Outcome
      Phase 1 2026-2027 Neural interface proof-of-concept Non-invasive brain-computer interface testing on paid volunteers Promising results. 73% of subjects showed enhanced reaction time
      Phase 2 2028 First human integration trials Invasive neural implants combining AI processing with human cognition 40% success rate. “Successful” subjects showed +340% physical capability. Failures resulted in catatonia or death
      Phase 3 2029 Forced trials Prisoners from Nexus black sites. No consent. Accelerated protocols Higher success rate (65%) but subjects showed increased aggression, reduced empathy, “hive-mind” behavior
      Phase 4 2030 Field deployment “Enhanced” subjects deployed as Nexus private security. Cover story: advanced exoskeleton program Subjects effective but increasingly uncontrollable. Internal reports suppressed
      Phase 5 Jan 2031 CONTAINMENT FAILURE Subject 7 broke containment. Chain reaction across all facilities All subjects escaped. Connection to central AI severed during cyberattack. Subjects became autonomous threats

      Subject Classification

      Designation Former Identity Enhancement Level Current Status In-Game Role
      Subject 7 Factory worker (name redacted) Extreme Active — Reactor Tower AI Boss: “Reactor Guardian”
      Subject 12 Dr. Viktor Chen (Project Lead) Self-administered, experimental Active — Tech Labs AI Boss: “The Scientist”
      Subject 19 Marcus Cole, Nexus Chief of Security Military-grade Active — Office Complex AI Boss: “The Warden”
      Subjects 1-6 Test volunteers (Phase 1) Minimal Deceased or dormant Environmental lore (corpses, audio logs)
      Subjects 8-11 Prisoners (Phase 3) Variable Unknown — scattered across NAIC Future content: roaming mini-bosses
      Subjects 13-18 Prisoners (Phase 3) Variable Deceased (containment protocols) Environmental lore
      Subjects 20+ Phase 4 deployments Standard military Unknown — may have integrated with factions Future content: NPC allies/enemies

      The Ethical Debate (Internal) 

      DR. CHEN:  "We need to stop. The neural degradation in Phase 3
           subjects is irreversible. We're creating weapons, not people."

DIRECTOR PARK: "The board has authorized Phase 4. Our mandate is clear."

DR. CHEN:  "Your mandate will create monsters."

DIRECTOR PARK: "Then we'll build better containment."

[Memo recovered from Lab Beta, dated October 2030]
[Handwritten note by Chen: "She knows. She doesn't care."]

      Design Note: Director Park later defected to found the Tech Syndicate. Her guilt about Prometheus is her hidden motivation — she seeks to expose Nexus’s crimes to atone for her role. This is revealed in Season 3.

      The Exclusion Zones (2031-2036)

      Phase 1: Chaos (2031-2032)

      The immediate aftermath of The Collapse:

      • No central authority — every community fends for itself
      • Contamination zones expand — Crimson Flu mutations, chemical plant leaks, radiation from damaged reactors
      • Enhanced subjects roam freely — unpredictable, territorial, increasingly inhuman
      • Survivor pockets form — based on proximity, profession, or pre-existing relationships
      • The first traders emerge — people who realize that barter between communities is profitable (and dangerous)

      Phase 2: Stabilization (2033-2034)

      Order begins to emerge from chaos:

      • Factions consolidate — The four main factions each control different resource types and territories
      • Trade routes established — Dangerous but essential paths between settlements
      • “Exclusion Zones” officially designated — Areas too contaminated, too dangerous, or too strategically valuable for permanent settlement
      • The NAIC becomes the largest Exclusion Zone — 5,000 km², partially contaminated, filled with salvageable technology and Nexus secrets

      Phase 3: Extraction Economy (2035-2036)

      The game begins here.

      • Contractors appear — Independent operators who venture into Exclusion Zones for profit
      • The black market matures — Structured economy around extracted goods
      • Factions compete for influence — Each faction wants different things from the Zone
      • The Broker emerges — A mysterious figure who controls information flow and contracts
      • Players enter the story — As newly arrived Contractors seeking fortune in the most dangerous place on Earth

      Regional History

      Sector 7: Industrial Decay (Raid Map)

      Aspect Pre-Collapse During Collapse Present Day
      Function Largest industrial zone in NAIC. 50,000 workers. Power plants, chemical processing, electronics manufacturing Day 1: Power loss. Day 3: Radiation leak from reactor. Day 7: Military seals perimeter. Day 14: Last survivor transmission. Day 30: Silence AI threats (enhanced subjects + automated systems). Radiation pockets. Valuable industrial salvage. Hidden Nexus secrets
      Key Locations Reactor Tower, Tech Labs (Alpha/Beta/Gamma), Warehouse District, Office Complex, Worker Dormitories Reactor became Subject 7’s territory. Labs were the origin of the Outbreak. Warehouses became smuggler havens Boss spawn locations, high-value loot zones, quest objective areas
      Atmosphere Buzzing activity, shift whistles, truck engines Explosions, screaming, then silence Creaking metal, dripping water, distant machinery still running on backup power

      What Players Will Find:

      • Audio logs from workers documenting their final days
      • Project Prometheus research notes (partially destroyed)
      • Nexus executive memos revealing the cover-up timeline
      • Subject 7’s containment cell (broken from the inside)
      • A functioning terminal with encrypted files (quest objective)

      District 14: Urban Ruins (Raid Map)

      Aspect Pre-Collapse During Collapse Present Day
      Function Commercial and residential hub. Population: 200,000. Shopping malls, apartments, hotels, subway system Week 1: Panic and looting. Week 2: Crimson Flu devastates dense population. Week 3: Military quarantine fails. Week 4: Partial evacuation. Month 2: Scavengers move in Underground Network territory. Survivor communities (hostile and friendly). Nature reclaiming the city. Subway is a dangerous shortcut
      Key Locations Central Mall, Residential Towers, Hotel Grand, Subway Station, Hospital (overrun) Mall became an improvised refugee camp. Hospital was the last to fall. Subway tunnels used for evacuation PvP hotspots (mall loot), NPC camps, underground passages, medical supply caches
      Atmosphere Neon lights, traffic, music, crowds Sirens, then silence, then weeping Wind through broken windows, birds nesting in towers, distant radio static

      What Players Will Find:

      • Survivor journals documenting the quarantine failure
      • Underground Network dead drops and coded messages
      • A functioning radio broadcasting on loop (NPC quest hook)
      • Evidence of Peacekeeper activities (both heroic and morally gray)
      • The “Last Doctor” — audio logs from a physician who stayed behind

      Firebase Delta: Military Outpost (Future Map)

      Aspect Pre-Collapse During Collapse Present Day
      Function Joint military research base, weapons testing facility, emergency command center Day 1: Activated as NAIC emergency HQ. Day 7: Overrun by enhanced subjects. Day 14: Nuclear fail-safe considered and rejected. Day 21: Abandoned, automated defenses left active Peacekeepers want to reclaim it. Automated turrets still functional. Prototype weapon caches. Classified intel about the true cause of The Collapse
      Key Locations Command Bunker, Weapons Lab, Airfield, Barracks, Underground Armory Command Bunker was the site of the “nuclear option” debate. Weapons Lab contains prototype tech Highest-difficulty map. Best military loot. Access to endgame story content
      Atmosphere Disciplined, orderly, purposeful Desperate, under siege, final transmissions Silent except for automated turret servos, wind across the empty airfield, distant echoes

      What Players Will Find:

      • The “Nuclear Option” recording — military leadership debating destroying the entire NAIC
      • Prototype weapons and armor (endgame gear)
      • Evidence connecting the military to Nexus Corp (they knew about Prometheus)
      • Commander Vance’s personal effects (she served here before founding the Peacekeepers)
      • Access codes to Firebase Delta’s underground armory (multi-session quest chain)

      Mysteries and Revelations

      Mystery Tracker Framework

      Each core mystery is revealed through a combination of channels. Percentages show how much of the truth each channel reveals:

      Mystery Audio Logs Quest Chains Environmental Total Reveal Season
      Who caused The Collapse? 30% 50% 20% 100% by Season 3 S1-S3
      The Broker’s identity 10% 40% 10% 60% by Season 4 S2-S4
      Is there a cure for Crimson Flu? 20% 60% 20% 100% by Season 4 S2-S4
      What happened to Dr. Chen? 40% 30% 30% 100% by Season 2 S1-S2
      What is Subject 7 becoming? 20% 30% 50% Ongoing S1+

      Red Herrings (Intentional Misdirection)

      To prevent players from solving mysteries too quickly, these deliberate false leads are planted:

      Red Herring What It Suggests What’s Actually True Where Found
      “USEC operative” dog tags Foreign military involvement in The Collapse Reference to a pre-Collapse private security company (unrelated) Factory floor, Sector 7
      Dr. Chen’s “suicide note” Chen killed himself out of guilt The note is forged. Chen is alive (Subject 12 = The Scientist boss) Lab Beta, locked drawer
      Peacekeeper internal memo about “pacification protocols” Peacekeepers are authoritarian villains The memo refers to legitimate quarantine enforcement, taken out of context Office Complex, Floor 4
      Underground coded message: “The Broker is in the tower” The Broker is hiding in the Reactor Tower “The tower” is code for a radio frequency, not a location Warehouse W3 dead drop

      Key Historical Figures

      Beyond faction leaders (detailed in Factions), these figures shaped the world:

      Name Role Status Significance
      Dr. Viktor Chen Lead Researcher, Project Prometheus Unknown (possibly Subject 12) Created the Enhancement Protocol. His research holds the key to a cure
      CFO James Thompson Nexus Corp Chief Financial Officer Deceased (Office Complex) Funded Prometheus. His financial records prove Nexus’s culpability
      General Li Wei NAIC Military Commander Deceased (Firebase Delta) Ordered the perimeter seal. His recordings contain the “nuclear option” debate
      “Nurse” Fatima Field medic, pre-Collapse hospital Alive (survivor camp, District 14) NPC quest giver. Holds medical knowledge critical for the cure storyline
      Sergeant Okafor Nexus security, Lab Alpha guard Deceased (Lab Alpha) His audio logs document Subject 7’s escape in real-time

      Lore Reveal Roadmap

      Season 1 (Launch)

      • The Collapse basics established through environmental storytelling
      • Introduction to 4 main factions via quest givers
      • Hints about Project Prometheus (audio logs, destroyed labs)
      • First 15-20 audio logs across Sector 7 (Industrial Decay)
      • Dr. Chen mystery begins — his office is found, but he is absent

      Season 2

      • The Outbreak detailed — Crimson Flu origins connected to Prometheus
      • Nexus Corporation history deepens through recovered financial records
      • Dr. Chen’s full story arc resolves (is he Subject 12?)
      • District 14 (Urban Ruins) adds 12-15 audio logs about civilian experience
      • The Broker begins communicating directly with high-reputation players

      Season 3

      • Major reveal: Who caused The Collapse (Nexus Corp’s decision chain exposed)
      • Director Park’s role revealed — Tech Syndicate shaken by the truth
      • Firebase Delta opens — military involvement in Prometheus confirmed
      • The Broker’s identity gets first concrete hints
      • Player quest choices begin to influence which faction gains territory

      Year 2+

      • Cure possibility explored through multi-season quest chain
      • New external threats emerge (other Exclusion Zones, surviving governments)
      • World expansion beyond NAIC
      • Player-driven narrative events (community votes, global quest outcomes)
      • The Broker identity and endgame narrative resolution
