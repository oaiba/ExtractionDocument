## 🗺️ Map Overview

*   **Name:** The Exclusion Mire (aka "The Swamp")
*   **Theme:** Flooded forest reclaiming a failed biological test site. Foggy, treacherous terrain.
*   **Size:** Large (2000m x 2000m)
*   **Player Count:** 16 Players (Duo/Squad Focus)
*   **Raid Time:** 30 Minutes
*   **Difficulty:** Expert (Map knowledge critical for survival)

**Unique Feature:** **Dynamic Water Level**. Water rises and recedes slowly. Lootable areas become deadly traps; new paths open up.

---

## 📍 Tactical Layout

The map is a **"Archipelago" design**: Small islands of dry land connected by shallow water (slows movement) or deep channels (requires swimming/boat).

```
   [Hills]      [Old Road]
      |            |
 [Crash Site]--[Swamp Water]--[Sunken Village]
      |            |             |
      |       [Deep Lake]        |
      |            |             |
 [Fishing Huts]---[BIODOME]----[Watchtower]
      |            (Lab)         |
      \____________|_____________/
              [Forest Mud]
```

### Zone 1: The Crash Site (West)
*Spawn area, basic loot.*

*   **1A. Plane Wreckage:** A crashed military transport. Cover is sparse but metal offers protection.
*   **1B. Fishing Huts:** Decaying wooden structures. Good for ambushes.
*   **1C. The Tree Line:** Dense foliage limiting visibility to 20m.

### Zone 2: The Marshlands (Center)
*Dangerous crossing, high risk of getting stuck.*

*   **2A. Sunken Village:** Roofs are the only safe ground. Falling into water = huge noise + slow.
*   **2B. The Watchtower:** Only high ground in the map. Perfect vision, zero cover.

### Zone 3: Bio-Dome Alpha (Hotspot)
*Sealed environment, chemical hazards.*

*   **Location:** Center of the deepest lake. Accessible by bridge (exposed) or underwater pipe (requires Rebreather).
*   **Layout:** Clean, white lab interiors contrasting with the muddy exterior.
*   **Threats:** Mutated AI wildlife (fast, melee focus).
*   **Loot:** Bio-samples, High-value medical injectors.

## 🛠️ Operator Tactical Interactions

Surviving nature requires specific skills:

| Class          | Interaction        | Benefit                                                      | Location Examples                                    |
| :------------- | :----------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------- |
| **SUPPORT**    | **Bridge Layer**   | Deploy a temporary plank bridge over mud/gaps.               | **Swamp Channels**: Cross silently without swimming. |
| **TANK**       | **Heavy Removal**  | Lift fallen logs blocking paths or loot.                     | **Forest Paths**: Open shortcuts.                    |
| **RECON**      | **Camouflage Net** | Set up a hidden observation post (invisible to thermal).     | **The Watchtower**: Ambush setup.                    |
| **SPECIALIST** | **Water Purify**   | Turn toxic water into a stamina-boosting drink (Consumable). | **Bio-Dome**: Heal effects of poison.                |

*   **Trap Crafting:** Recons can rig tripwires on narrow swamp paths.

## ⚔️ Choke Points & Flow

### 1. "The Causeway" (Bridge to Bio-Dome)
*   **Description:** A long, straight concrete bridge. No cover except broken cars.
*   **Tactical:** Suicide run for squads without smoke grenades.
*   **Danger:** Any sniper in the Watchtower controls this.

### 2. "Alligator Alley" (Shallow Channel)
*   **Description:** A narrow path through thick reeds.
*   **Tactical:** The stealthy approach.
*   **Danger:** Getting stuck in mud slows movement by 50%. Easy pickings for grenades.

## 🎨 Environmental Design

*   **Palette:** Mud Brown, Sickly Green, Fog Grey.
*   **Auditory Illusion:** Sounds carry differently in fog. Gunshots sound closer than they are.
*   **Hazards:** Mutated leeches in water (Drain Stamina), Gas pockets.
