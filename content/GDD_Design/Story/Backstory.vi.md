### Pre-Collapse Era (2020-2030)

#### The Age of Instability (2020-2025)

The world did not collapse overnight. It eroded.

| Year      | Event                                         | Consequence                                                                                                                                |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 2020-2021 | COVID-19 pandemic                             | global supply chains fracture. Remote work normalizes. Public trust in institutions drops by 40%                                           |
| 2022      | Energy crisis (Ukraine conflict escalation)   | European energy markets collapse. Fuel prices triple. Rationing begins in 12 countries                                                     |
| 2023      | "The Great Resignation" reaches critical mass | Labor shortages in critical infrastructure (hospitals, power plants, logistics). Automation accelerates                                    |
| 2024      | AI Act failures                               | Regulatory frameworks lag behind AI capabilities. Deepfakes destabilize elections in 6 nations                                             |
| 2025      | "The Water Wars" begin                        | Freshwater scarcity triggers border conflicts in Central Asia, North Africa, và the American Southwest. The UN Security Council deadlocks |

**Design Note**: These events are recognizable extensions of real-world trends. This grounds the fiction in plausibility — Người chơi nên feel "this could happen."

**The Neo-Asia Industrial Corridor (NAIC)**

The NAIC was Nexus Corp's flagship project — a massive manufacturing và technology hub spanning a fictional region inspired by East và Southeast Asian urban sprawl.

**Concept:**

* A Special Economic Zone với **zero government oversight** — Nexus was the law
* Population: 3 million workers relocated với promises of employment và housing
* Infrastructure: Power plants, chemical processing, electronics manufacturing, residential towers, commercial districts
* **Hidden mục đích**: Provide cover for Project Prometheus research facilities

**Inspirations:**

* Shenzhen's explosive growth (speed of development)
* Korean DMZ aesthetic (militarized perimeter)
* Japanese industrial decay (abandoned factories)
* Southeast Asian urban density (vertical sprawl, mixed-cách dùng chaos)

### The Collapse (2031)

#### The Triangle of Disaster

Three simultaneous crises created a cascading failure that no single institution could contain:

```
           CYBERATTACK
           Infrastructure paralyzed
              /          \
             /   PERFECT   \
            /    STORM      \
           /                 \
      PANDEMIC          RESOURCE WAR
      15-20% mortality  Nations fracture
```

#### chi tiết Event Chain

| Date   | Event                                                                                     | Immediate Consequence                                                                        | Long-term Impact                                                                     |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Jan 15 | **global Blackout** — Coordinated cyberattack on power infrastructure across 47 countries | 72-hour total power loss in major cities. Hospitals switch to generators. Internet goes dark | Permanent distrust of digital infrastructure. Cash economy resurges briefly          |
| Jan 20 | **Banking hệ thống Crash** — Financial networks data corrupted beyond repair                | Economic collapse. Savings evaporate. Stock markets never reopen                             | Currency becomes meaningless. Barter economy emerges within weeks                    |
| Feb 1  | **"Crimson Flu" appears** — Novel pathogen in NAIC region                                 | Initial containment attempted. WHO declares emergency                                        | Origin suspect: Project Prometheus lab leak (never officially confirmed)             |
| Feb 14 | **Quarantine fails** — Crimson Flu crosses containment perimeter                          | global spread within 2 weeks. 15-20% mortality rate. Hospitals collapse                      | No healthcare hệ thống survives. Medical knowledge becomes the most valuable commodity |
| Mar 1  | **Food riots** — Supply chain collapse reaches critical point                             | Supermarkets looted within hours. Farms militarized. Water treatment plants abandoned        | Starvation becomes the primary killer, surpassing the virus by Month 3               |
| Mar 15 | **Military splinters** — Chain of command breaks as soldiers prioritize families          | Desertion rate hits 60%. Remaining units form warlord factions                               | Organized violence replaces organized law. The strong take from the weak             |
| Apr 1  | **Governments fall** — Last functioning trạng thái dissolve                                   | No more borders, laws, hoặc emergency services                                                 | The old world is officially dead                                                     |
| Apr 15 | **The Collapse complete** — global civilization as a unified hệ thống ceases to exist       | Isolated pockets of order remain (military bases, corporate fortresses, island communities)  | The Exclusion Zone era begins                                                        |

#### Who Caused It?

**The truth (revealed gradually across Seasons 1-3):**

Nobody "caused" The Collapse by themselves. nhưng Nexus Corporation was the primary catalyst:

1. **Project Prometheus** created enhanced human subjects whose containment failure may have triggered hoặc accelerated the Crimson Flu pandemic
2. Nexus's SafeNet infrastructure had **backdoors that hackers exploited** for the cyberattack — vulnerabilities Nexus knew about nhưng chose not to patch (it would have reduced data collection)
3. Nexus's monopolization of critical infrastructure meant **no redundancy existed** khi hệ thống failed

**The uncomfortable truth**: The Collapse was preventable. Nexus chose profit over safety at every quyết định point.

### Project Prometheus (2026-2031)

#### Program Overview

```
PROJECT PROMETHEUS — "Enhancement Through Integration"
Classification: NEXUS LEVEL BLACK
Location: NAIC Tech Labs (Labs Alpha, Beta, Gamma)
Director: Dr. Seo-Yun Park (later defected → Tech Syndicate leader)
Lead Researcher: Dr. Viktor Chen (deceased? — conflicting reports)
Budget: $4.7 billion (hidden across 12 subsidiary ledgers)
```

#### Research Phases

| Phase   | Years     | Objective                         | Method                                                                                            | Outcome                                                                                                           |
| ------- | --------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Phase 1 | 2026-2027 | Neural interface proof-of-concept | Non-invasive brain-computer interface testing on paid volunteers                                  | Promising results. 73% of subjects showed enhanced reaction thời gian                                                  |
| Phase 2 | 2028      | First human integration trials    | Invasive neural implants combining AI processing với human cognition                             | 40% success rate. "Successful" subjects showed +340% physical capability. Failures resulted in catatonia hoặc death |
| Phase 3 | 2029      | Forced trials                     | Prisoners from Nexus black sites. No consent. Accelerated protocols                               | Higher success rate (65%) nhưng subjects showed increased aggression, reduced empathy, "hive-mind" behavior         |
| Phase 4 | 2030      | Field deployment                  | "Enhanced" subjects deployed as Nexus private security. Cover story: advanced exoskeleton program | Subjects effective nhưng increasingly uncontrollable. Internal reports suppressed                                   |
| Phase 5 | Jan 2031  | **CONTAINMENT FAILURE**           | Subject 7 broke containment. Chain reaction across all facilities                                 | All subjects escaped. Connection to central AI severed trong khi cyberattack. Subjects became autonomous threats     |

### The Exclusion Zones (2031-2036)

#### Phase 1: Chaos (2031-2032)

The immediate aftermath of The Collapse:

* **No central authority** — every community fends for itself
* **Contamination zones expand** — Crimson Flu mutations, chemical plant leaks, radiation from damaged reactors
* **Enhanced subjects roam freely** — unpredictable, territorial, increasingly inhuman
* **Survivor pockets form** — based on proximity, profession, hoặc pre-existing relationships
* **The first traders emerge** — people who realize that barter between communities is profitable (và dangerous)

#### Phase 2: Stabilization (2033-2034)

Order begins to emerge from chaos:

* **Factions consolidate** — The four main factions each control different resource types và territories
* **Trade routes established** — Dangerous nhưng essential paths between settlements
* **"Exclusion Zones" officially designated** — Areas too contaminated, too dangerous, hoặc too strategically valuable for permanent settlement
* **The NAIC becomes the largest Exclusion Zone** — 5,000 km², partially contaminated, filled với salvageable technology và Nexus secrets

#### Phase 3: Extraction Economy (2035-2036)

The game begins here.

* **Contractors appear** — Independent operators who venture into Exclusion Zones for profit
* **The black market matures** — Structured economy around extracted goods
* **Factions compete for influence** — Each faction wants different things from the Zone
* **The Broker emerges** — A mysterious figure who controls information flow và contracts
* **người chơi enter the story** — As newly arrived Contractors seeking fortune in the most dangerous place on Earth

### Lore Reveal Roadmap

#### Season 1 (Launch)

* The Collapse basics established thông qua environmental storytelling
* Introduction to 4 main factions via quest givers
* Hints about Project Prometheus (audio logs, destroyed labs)
* First 15-20 audio logs across Sector 7 (Industrial Decay)
* Dr. Chen mystery begins — his office is found, nhưng he is absent

#### Season 2

* The Outbreak chi tiết — Crimson Flu origins connected to Prometheus
* Nexus Corporation history deepens thông qua recovered financial records
* Dr. Chen's full story arc resolves (is he Subject 12?)
* District 14 (Urban Ruins) adds 12-15 audio logs about civilian trải nghiệm
* The Broker begins communicating directly với high-reputation người chơi

#### Season 3

* **Major reveal**: Who caused The Collapse (Nexus Corp's quyết định chain exposed)
* Director Park's role revealed — Tech Syndicate shaken by the truth
* Firebase Delta opens — military involvement in Prometheus confirmed
* The Broker's identity gets first concrete hints
* người chơi quest choices begin to influence which faction gains territory

#### Year 2+

* Cure possibility explored thông qua multi-season quest chain
* New external threats emerge (other Exclusion Zones, surviving governments)
* World expansion beyond NAIC
* người chơi-driven narrative events (community votes, global quest outcomes)
* The Broker identity và endgame narrative resolution
