## 🤝 Beyond the Gun

Extraction shooters thrive on player interaction. It's not just about PvP; it's about forming temporary alliances, betraying trust, and squad coordination.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
**Core Tenet:** Every social interaction should have friction. Trust is earned, not given by a UI element.
{{< /callout >}}

### Squad Mechanics
*   **VoIP:** Proximity voice chat (hear enemies).
*   **Revive System:** High-risk, high-reward. Requires vulnerability.
*   **Loot Sharing:** No "need/greed" rolls. Drop it on the ground.
