## Overview

This document defines how the 10 operators interact with each other — both within a squad (**synergies**) and against opponents (**counters**). A deep synergy and counter system is the foundation of a meta-game that stays fresh across seasons. Players should be able to say "I want to try Hawk + Ghost + Glitch combo" and have that feel meaningfully different from "Mamba + Bastion + Fuse."

> **Cross-References:** [Hero Abilities](hero_abilities/index.html) — full ability specs for all 10 operators; [Downstate & Revive](downstate_revive/index.html) — Ghost's Field Medic revive mechanic; [LOS, Fog & Visibility](los_fog_visibility/index.html) — Hawk/Glitch vision abilities referenced throughout; [Combat Feel](combat_feel_topdown/index.html) — suppression, aim cone interactions with abilities.

## Synergy Design Principles

| Principle | Detail |
| :-------- | :----- |
| **No mandatory combo** | Every squad of 3 should be viable with any combination. Synergies are multipliers, not requirements. |
| **Synergies are discovered, not prescribed** | Players finding a "secret" 3-operator combo should feel clever. Don't meta-lock the roster via game pressures. |
| **Counter-play always exists** | Every 2-3 operator combo should have at least one operator or tactic that breaks it effectively. |
| **Solo-viable operators don't need synergy** | Phaedra, Obsidian, and Fuse are designed to be strong solo — they synergize but don't depend on teammates. |

## Tier 1: Best-in-Slot Synergies

### 1. "The Eyes & Ears" — Hawk + Glitch + Mamba

**Squad Role:** Total information dominance + aggressive execution.

| Ability Combination | Effect |
| :------------------ | :----- |
| Hawk's **Spotter Drone** reveals enemy position | Shared minimap vision updated 25m drone radius |
| Glitch's **Tactical Overlay** covers indoor positions or last-known | Combined intel reveals cover positions |
| Mamba's **Adrenaline Rush** executes the push on revealed targets | High-speed entry from known angle |

**How it works:**
1. Hawk deploys drone 30m ahead of squad's push path.
2. Glitch activates Tactical Overlay on the building entrance.
3. Combined vision: squad sees 3–5 enemy positions before entering.
4. Mamba sprints in on Adrenaline Rush targeting the weakest link.

**Counter:** This combo relies on drone being alive. An enemy who hears the drone and shoots it (30 HP) breaks Hawk's intel. Against Phaedra — her Steel Sight during the entry ignores Mamba's armor mid-push.

---

### 2. "The Bunker" — Bastion + Fuse + Ghost

**Squad Role:** Goliath extraction defense — hold a point until extraction.

| Ability Combination | Effect |
| :------------------ | :----- |
| Fuse plants **Trip Mines** at all approach corridors | Zone denial; enemies must prone-crawl (slow) |
| Bastion activates **Goliath Protocol** | 50% damage reduction in extraction zone |
| Ghost provides **Mass Stabilize** if squad takes heavy damage | Bleed stop; enables continued holding |

**How it works:**
1. Squad arrives at extraction zone 90s early.
2. Fuse places 3 Trip Mine charges at the 2–3 approach paths.
3. Bastion stands at center, activating Goliath at first contact.
4. Ghost stays back, watching all three for downstate.

**Counter:** Obsidian's Phase Shift bypasses standard LOS — can slip behind Bastion's directional shield. Phaedra's Spotter Round tags Bastion through the shield from 25m+, giving enemy team exact position for flanking.

---

### 3. "Ghost Protocol" — Obsidian + Hawk + Ghost

**Squad Role:** Stealth insertion + guaranteed revive + persistent intel.

| Ability Combination | Effect |
| :------------------ | :----- |
| Obsidian's **Ghost Step** passive | Entire squad benefits indirectly — Obsidian leads; enemies focus on her; others follow in noise shadow |
| Hawk's **Motion Sensor** placed at backtrack points | Alerts squad if enemy follows the flanking approach |
| Ghost's **Field Medic** (5s revive) | If Obsidian or Hawk is caught and downed, Ghost recovers them twice before cooldown |

**Counter:** Hawk's Motion Sensor is countered by enemies going prone (slow-crawl). Ghost's position gives away the squad when he must move to revive — enemy team camping the downed player can bait Ghost out.

---

### 4. "Iron Curtain" — Ironclad + Bastion + Glitch

**Squad Role:** Aggressive area control — push through buildings, clear floor by floor.

| Ability Combination | Effect |
| :------------------ | :----- |
| Ironclad's **Armor Slam** destroys doors | Instant room breach — no door interaction needed |
| Bastion's **Goliath Protocol** on the breached room | Entry team protected mid-breach |
| Glitch's **Tech Savvy** detects traps on the other side | Pre-breach trap clear — counters Fuse specifically |

**Counter:** Any squad running Fuse — Glitch's Tech Savvy doesn't defuse mines, just reveals them. A Fuse who placed mines BEFORE Glitch's scan has window advantage. Phaedra can hold the corridor Ironclad must sprint through; Steel Sight makes Ironclad's armor irrelevant.

## Tier 2: Situational Synergies

### 5. "Sniper's Nest" — Phaedra + Hawk + Ghost

| Synergy | Detail |
| :------ | :----- |
| Hawk's **Spotter Round** tags enemy | Phaedra sees tagged enemy on minimap — long-range confirm |
| Hawk's drone scouts movement patterns | Phaedra pre-positions for predictive shot |
| Ghost's **Mass Stabilize** covers Phaedra if she gets flanked | Phaedra's main weakness (no mobility) offset by Ghost stabilizing from bleed |

**Best for:** Large open maps; boss fights from safe distance.

---

### 6. "The Trade" — Mamba + Fuse

| Synergy | Detail |
| :------ | :----- |
| Mamba pushes aggressively, draws fire | Enemies focused on Mamba step into Fuse's Trip Mines |
| Fuse's Minefield provides emergency extraction zone defense | Mamba retreats into minefield if overwhelmed — enemies cannot follow |

**Best for:** Duos; aggressive extraction zone camping counter.

---

### 7. "Solo Apex" — Phaedra or Obsidian (Solo Queue)

Neither of these operators require squad synergy, making them the strongest solo-queue operators:

| Solo Operator | Why It Works Solo |
| :------------ | :---------------- |
| **Phaedra** | Never needs a teammate to reveal targets (Spotter Round self-sufficient); long range keeps her out of revive requirement |
| **Obsidian** | Phase Shift + Ghost Step = self-sufficient stealth evasion; Decoy Ping creates own distractions; Smoke grenade provides own cover |

## Counter Matrix

The following table shows which operators **counter** which. A "hard counter" means one operator's kit directly neutralizes the other's main strength.

| Operator Being Countered | Hard Counter | Counter Ability/Tactic | Why |
| :----------------------- | :----------- | :--------------------- | :-- |
| **Hawk** (Drone) | Any player with weapons | Shoot drone (30 HP) | No drone = no intel; Hawk loses main value |
| **Hawk** (Motion Sensor) | Prone movement | Slow crawl past sensor | Fuse/Obsidian players who know sensor placement avoid trigger |
| **Glitch** (traps) | Ironclad | Armor Slam + Tech Savvy combo | Reveals all traps before breach |
| **Bastion** (Goliath) | Phaedra (Steel Sight) | Armor bypass | Goliath reduces damage — Steel Sight ignores armor entirely |
| **Bastion** (Goliath) | Obsidian (flank) | Phase Shift around shield arc | Goliath is directional — flanking negates it |
| **Ghost** (revive) | Fuse (Trip Mine at downed player) | Mine bait | Ghost must approach downed player — mine catches reviver |
| **Fuse** (Trip Mine) | Prone movement | All mines triggered by walking/sprinting only | Veteran players prone past mines |
| **Fuse** (Minefield) | Glitch (Tech Savvy) | Mines revealed | Glitch saves squad from minefield zone entry |
| **Phaedra** (long range) | Mamba (Adrenaline Rush into her) | Instant close-range rush | Phaedra has no escape — closed-to-short range is her dead zone |
| **Phaedra** (Steel Sight) | Cover | Any hard cover | Steel Sight bypasses armor but wall geometry still blocks bullets |
| **Ironclad** (Armor Slam) | Glitch (Tech Savvy pre-scan) | Detect mines behind door | Reveals Fuse setups before Ironclad breaches |
| **Obsidian** (Phase Shift) | Hawk (Motion Sensor) | Motion detected through cloak | Motion Sensor detects Obsidian — smart Hawk counters Obsidian |
| **Obsidian** (Ghost Step) | Sound-cancelling not = invisible | Top-down LOS | Obsidian is still visible — just quiet. Camera reveals position if in LOS. |

| **Sustained Hold** | Bastion + Ghost + Fuse | Extraction zone fortification |

| **Solo Queue** | Phaedra or Obsidian | Self-sufficient; least squad-dependent |

## Balance Guardrails

To prevent meta lock-in:

| Guardrail | Implementation |
| :-------- | :------------- |
| **No passive stacks** | Two operators with movement buffs do NOT stack (higher value applies, not both). |
| **Ability cooldowns independent** | Two Ghosts in a squad both have full revive ability — this is intentional (double medic meta). |
| **Drone range cap** | Two Hawk players' drones DO share minimap data — but map exploit is prevented by server-capping drone duration at 45s. |
| **Signature abilities**: non-stackable AoE | Two Bulwarks cannot overlap Goliath Protocols to create permanent immunity zones — each Goliath is personal (applies only to the casting operator). |
| **Balancing philosophy** | Monthly balance audits using play-rate data. Any operator >40% squad pick rate triggers a balance review. |
