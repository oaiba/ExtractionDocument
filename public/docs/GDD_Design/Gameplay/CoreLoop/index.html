    Core Gameplay Loop & Progression#

    🔁 The Extraction Loop (The “Cycle”)#

    The core engagement loop is designed to create a “Hero’s Journey” in miniature, built on the cycle of Risk Assessment -> Execution -> Consequence.

    1. Preparation Phase (Safety & Investment)#

    • Psychology: Loss Aversion. The more gear you equip, the more confident you feel, but the more you fear death.
    • Key Decisions:
      • Budget vs. Chad: “Do I bring my best M4A1 to survive, or a cheap shotgun to minimize loss?”
      • Objective Selection: “Do I hunt players (High Risk) or loot the perimeter (Low Risk)?”
      • Consumable Tetris: Packing food, water, and meds. Balancing utility vs. inventory space for loot.

    2. Infiltration Phase (Tension & Information)#

    • Psychology: Sensory Overload.
    • Mechanics:
      • The Spawn: Random insertion. immediate check of surroundings.
      • Sound Discipline: Moving slowly to hear enemies first. Silence is your radar.
      • Map Knowledge: Recognizing landmarks to orient without a minimap.

    3. Execution Phase (The “Peak”)#

    • Psychology: Flow State.
    • Combat: Millisecond decisions. Flanking, suppressing, grenade usage.
    • Looting: The dopamine hit of finding a high-value item (GPU, Keycard).
    • The “Greed” Check: “I found a quest item. Should I leave NOW, or check one more room?”

    4. Extraction Phase (The “Climax”)#

    • Psychology: Peak Anxiety.
    • Mechanics:
      • The Exfil timer: A 10-60 second countdown where you are defenseless or exposed.
      • Exit Camping: The threat of enemies watching the exit.
      • Success: The splash screen showing XP and Loot Value. The release of tension.

    5. Recovery Phase (The “Aftermath”)#

    • Psychology: Analysis & Growth.
    • Actions:
      • Healing: Treating wounds that persist post-raid (optional mechanic).
      • Tetris: Organizing the stash.
      • The Market: Selling loot to fund the next run.

    🧠 Psychological Hooks & Logic#

    Why do players come back?

    Hook Concept Implementation
    Variable Ratio Reinforcement “The Slot Machine” Loot containers have random contents. You pull the lever (open the box) hoping for a Jackpot. Most times it’s junk, but the chance keeps you searching.
    Loss Aversion “It’s Mine” Once you loot an item, you feel ownership. Dying feels like having it stolen. This anger drives the “One More Game” mentality to reclaim losses.
    Sunk Cost Fallacy “I’m Committed” Upgrading the Hideout requires massive resource investment. Players play to “justify” the time/money already spent.
    Mastery Curve “I’m Getting Better” Learning map angles and recoil control is a permanent skill that persists through death/wipes.

    📊 Advanced Economy: Faucets & Sinks#

    To prevent hyper-inflation (where everyone has the best gear), we use a strict Faucet/Sink model.

    🚰 Faucets (Resource Inflow)#

    These generate value in the economy.

    1. Raid Loot: The primary source. Items spawned by the server.
    2. Scav Mode: Free “welfare” runs with zero risk, injecting free gear into the economy.
    3. Bitcoin Farm (Passive): Hideout modules that generate currency over time (must be capped/balanced).
    4. Trader Resets: Infinite supply of basic ammo/meds from NPCs.

    🚽 Sinks (Resource Destruction)#

    These remove value to maintain scarcity.

    1. Deleted on Death: Uninsured gear is deleted if not looted by another player.
    2. Consumables: Ammo, Meds, Food, and Grenades are one-time use. High-tier ammo is a massive money sink.
    3. Insurance Fees: Paying 10-20% of item value for a chance to get it back.
    4. Flea Market Tax: A progressive tax on selling items to other players.
    5. Hideout Upgrades: Massive material dumps required to unlock passive bonuses.
    6. Repair Degradation: Armor loses max durability when repaired, eventually becoming trash.

    Dynamic Balancing (The “Invisible Hand”)#

    • Trader Barters: If “Gunpowder” becomes too common/cheap, Traders will demand more Gunpowder for high-tier trades, artificially increasing demand.
    • Scarcity Events: “Fuel Shortage” events reduce spawn rates of fuel, draining cash reserves as players panic-buy.
