# 🏗️ Building Boundaries

Defining what the game **IS** is as important as defining what it is **NOT**. This section outlines the boundaries of our development to prevent feature creep and ensure a focused MVP.

## Genre Positioning

**Multiplayer hero shooter looter extraction, top-down perspective, cross-platform.** Session length 15–20 minutes (mobile-friendly). Free-to-play with fair monetization (no pay-to-win). Operators with classes and abilities; grid inventory, weight, and extraction loop with risk-of-loss and persistent progression. See [Scope Review & Planning](https://github.com/oaiba/ExtractionDocument/blob/main/content/GDD_Design/ProjectScope/project-scope-review-and-planning.html) for benchmarks and quarterly roadmap alignment.
