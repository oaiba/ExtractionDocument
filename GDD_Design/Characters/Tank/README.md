## Class Overview

The **Tank** class operators absorb damage and protect teammates. They excel at holding positions and leading pushes with their superior survivability.

### Class Identity

| Attribute           | Value         | Notes               |
| :------------------ | :------------ | :------------------ |
| **Role**            | Damage Sponge | Absorb and protect  |
| **Difficulty**      | ★★☆☆☆         | Beginner-friendly   |
| **Team Dependency** | Medium        | Better with team    |
| **Skill Ceiling**   | Medium        | Positioning crucial |

### Class Traits (All Tank Operators)

| Trait                | Effect                | Gameplay Impact  |
| :------------------- | :-------------------- | :--------------- |
| **Reinforced Armor** | +25% Maximum Armor    | 125 armor cap    |
| **Damage Reduction** | +10% Armor Absorption | Take less damage |
| **Heavy Frame**      | -15% Sprint Speed     | Slow rotations   |
