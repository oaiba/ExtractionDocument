##  Sonic Vision

In an extraction shooter, audio is **survival**. Sound design must communicate information first, and immersion second. Every footstep, reload, and distant gunshot tells a story about the threat level in the immediate area.

Our audio pillars focus on **clarity**, **positional accuracy**, and **tactical feedback**.

##  Key Audio Features

### 1. Occulsion & Propagation
Sound must behave realistically.
*   **Indoor/Outdoor:** Drastic EQ shifts when moving between environments.
*   **Verticality:** Clear distinction between sounds above and below the player.

### 2. The "Info Layer"
Critical gameplay sounds (footsteps, reloads, pin pulls) cut through the mix.
*   **High Priority:** Enemy movement within 30m.
*   **Medium Priority:** Gunfire (distant), explosions.
*   **Low Priority:** Ambient wind, machinery hum.

### 3. Tension Building
Silence is as important as noise. The sudden cessation of ambient wildlife can indicate a nearby predator or player.
