## Overview

The narrative design for this extraction shooter uses **three storytelling modes** that layer to create a rich, discoverable world:

| Mode | Description | Player Agency |
| :--- | :---------- | :------------ |
| **Implicit** | World tells its story through environment, props, and atmosphere | Passive — player observes |
| **Explicit** | NPCs, quests, audio logs, and documents deliver lore | Active — player engages |
| **Emergent** | Player-to-player interactions create unique stories | Organic — unscripted |

**Setting**: The Neo-Asia Industrial Corridor (NAIC) — a massive Exclusion Zone, five years after the collapse of global civilization.

> **Cross-references**: For environmental storytelling art direction, see [World / Environmental Narrative](../World/). For individual operator profiles, see [Characters & Operators](../Characters/).
