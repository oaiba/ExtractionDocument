## Map Overview

*   **Name:** Sector 7 Industrial Complex (aka "The Factory")
*   **Theme:** Abandoned Soviet-era processing plant repurposed for anomalous research.
*   **Size:** Medium (1200m x 1200m)
*   **người chơi Count:** 12 người chơi (Solo/Duo/Squads) + 20 AI Bots
*   **Raid thời gian:** 20 Minutes
*   **Difficulty:** Intermediate (Balanced Loot/Risk)

**Narrative Context:**
Once the primary refining facility for local crystalline resources. khi the "Outbreak" occurred, the military sealed the exits, trapping workers inside. Now, it's a scavenger's paradise filled với raw materials, left-behind military gear, và dangerous chemical leaks.

## Tactical Layout

The map is a **"Bowl" design**: High ground on the perimeter (Forest/Hills), low ground in the center (Factory Complex), promoting inward movement.

```
       [Forest/Hills] (High Ground)
      /      |       \
     /   [Train Yard] \
    |         |        |
[Storage]--[BRIDGE]--[OFFICES]
    |         |        |
    |      [FACTORY]---|
    |      (Low/Hot)   |
[Parking]----|----[Main Gate]
    |        |         |
    \    [Perimeter]   /
     \_______|________/
```

### Zone 1: The Perimeter (Green Zone)
*Safe start, low loot, long sightlines.*

*   **1A. Train Yard (North):** Rusted cargo trains. Good linear cover. Sniper vantage points on train cars.
*   **1B. Storage Units (West):** Rows of garage-style storage. Close-quarters combat (CQC). "Rat" looting spots.
*   **1C. Parking Lot (South):** Abandoned civilian cars. Open area, hazardous to cross.

### Zone 2: The Complex (Yellow Zone)
*Moderate risk, industrial combat, verticality.*

*   **2A. Assembly Hall:** Huge open interior với conveyor belts. 2 levels (Ground + Catwalks). High verticality.
*   **2B. Offices:** 3-story brick building attached to the hall. Tight corridors, breaching doors, filing cabinets (Intel loot).
*   **2C. Cooling Towers:** Two massive towers. External spiral stairs leading to high ground sniping spots.

### Zone 3: The Lab (Red Zone / Hotspot)
*Extreme risk, highest tier loot, Boss spawn.*

*   **Location:** Underground, accessible via 2A Assembly Hall elevator shaft hoặc 2B Office basement.
*   **Layout:** Claustrophobic white corridors, broken glass, flickering lights.
*   **Threats:** High-level AI guards, The "Warden" (Boss).
*   **Loot:** Keycards, Injectors, High-tech vũ khí parts.

## Choke Points & flow

Extraction shooters live và die by their Choke Points - areas where người chơi are *forced* to interact.

### 1. "The Bridge" (Connecting West Storage to Factory)
*   **Description:** A narrow metal skybridge over a chemical canal.
*   **Tactical:** The only direct path from West Spawn to the Hotspot mà không going thông qua the open canal (slow movement).
*   **Danger:** High. Easy to camp from the Office windows.

### 2. "Main Gate" (Factory Entrance)
*   **Description:** The primary reinforced gate into the compound. Slightly open, tạo a funnel.
*   **Tactical:** High traffic area for vehicles (future) hoặc squads rushing the center.
*   **Danger:** Variable. AI Scavs often patrol here.

### 3. "The Vent" (Lab Entrance)
*   **Description:** A destructible ventilation grate leading into the Lab.
*   **Tactical:** Requires explosives hoặc a crowbar to open. Noisy entry.
*   **Danger:** Extreme. "Dinner bell" for nearby người chơi.

## Environmental Design & Atmosphere

### Visual Identity
*   **Palette:** Rust Orange, Concrete Grey, Toxic Green (Chemicals).
*   **Lighting:** Overcast day (Standard). Interiors are dim, requiring flashlights/NVGs in the Lab.
*   **chi tiết:**
    *   *Storytelling:* "giúp Us" painted on the roof.
    *   *Assets:* Forklifts, pallets of rotting supplies, military checkpoints.

### Audio Landscape
*   **Ambient:** Distant humming of the generator (directional clue), dripping water in tunnels, crows cawing outside.
*   **Surfaces:**
    *   *Metal (Catwalks):* Loud, clanky. punishment for sprinting.
    *   *Glass (Offices):* Crunching noise (Crucial for hearing flankers).
    *   *Puddles (Canal):* Splashing sounds.

## Evolution Plan (Live Ops)

*   **Phase 1 (Launch):** Ground floor & 1st floor only. Lab sealed.
*   **Phase 2:** The "Lab" opens. New Boss added.
*   **Phase 3:** Dynamic Event - "Chemical Leak" floods the lower tunnels, forcing người chơi to high ground.
