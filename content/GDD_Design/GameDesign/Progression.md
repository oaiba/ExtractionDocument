## Progression Philosophy

**Core Principles:**
1. **Meaningful Progress** - Every match advances something
2. **Multiple Paths** - Cater to different playstyles
3. **Fair Free-to-Play** - Never pay-to-win
4. **Respect Time** - No excessive grinding
5. **Long-term Goals** - Keep players engaged for months

---

## Player Account Levels

### Level System

**Max Level:** 100 (then Prestige)

**XP Sources:**
- Match completion: 100 XP (base)
- Successful extraction: +200 XP
- Kills: +50 XP each
- Assists: +25 XP each
- Survival time: +10 XP per minute
- Loot extracted: +1 XP per $100 value
- Quest completion: Variable (100-1000 XP)
- Daily login: +50 XP

**XP Requirements:**
```
Level 1→2:   1,000 XP
Level 2→3:   1,200 XP
Level 10→11: 2,500 XP
Level 25→26: 5,000 XP
Level 50→51: 10,000 XP
Level 99→100: 25,000 XP

Total to 100: ~1,500,000 XP
Estimated time: 150-200 hours
```

---

### Level Rewards

**Every Level:**
- Credits: 500-2,000 (scaling)
- Cosmetic items (select levels)
- Operator unlocks (levels 5, 10, 15)

**Milestone Rewards:**

**Level 5:**
- Unlock Recon Operator
- 5,000 Credits
- Rare weapon skin

**Level 10:**
- Unlock Tank Operator
- 10,000 Credits
- Epic gear piece

**Level 15:**
- Unlock Specialist Operator
- Rare loot container key
- Character emote

**Level 25:**
- Epic weapon skin
- 25,000 Credits
- Stash expansion (+10 slots)

**Level 50:**
- Legendary weapon skin
- 50,000 Credits
- Name plate customization

**Level 100:**
- Prestige option
- Exclusive title
- 100,000 Credits
- Legendary operator skin

---

### Prestige System

**What is Prestige:**
- Reset to Level 1 (keep all items/operators)
- New prestige icon
- Exclusive cosmetics per prestige
- Small permanent stat bonuses

**Prestige Levels:** 1-10

**Prestige Rewards:**

**Prestige 1:**
- Golden name border
- +2% XP gain (permanent)
- Exclusive weapon skin

**Prestige 5:**
- Diamond name border
- +5% XP gain (total)
- Legendary operator outfit

**Prestige 10 (Max):**
- Rainbow name border
- +10% XP gain (total)
- Exclusive "Veteran" title
- Unique emote
- Early access to new content

## Operator Progression

### Individual Operator Levels

**Max Level per Operator:** 50

**XP Gain:**
- Only when using that operator
- Same sources as account XP
- Independent progression

**Level Milestones:**

**Level 5:** Ability upgrade slot 1
**Level 10:** Cosmetic skin 1
**Level 15:** Stat boost (+5% health)
**Level 20:** Ability upgrade slot 2
**Level 25:** Cosmetic skin 2 (rare)
**Level 30:** Stat boost (+5% stamina)
**Level 35:** Ability upgrade slot 3
**Level 40:** Cosmetic skin 3 (epic)
**Level 45:** Stat boost (+5% sprint speed)
**Level 50:** Prestige skin (legendary)

---

### Operator Mastery

**After Level 50:**
- Mastery points (infinite progression)
- Mastery ranks: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Diamond
- Purely cosmetic bragging rights
- Operator-specific badges

**Mastery Rewards:**
- Bronze (1,000 pts): Unique execution animation
- Silver (5,000 pts): Weapon skin set
- Gold (15,000 pts): Legendary outfit
- Diamond (50,000 pts): Unique victory pose + title

## Economy System

### Currency Types

#### 1. Credits (Soft Currency)

**How to Earn:**
- Extract loot: Sell for credits
- Level-up rewards
- Quest completions
- Daily login streak
- Match performance bonuses

**What to Buy:**
- Common/Uncommon weapons
- Common armor
- Consumables
- Operator unlocks
- Cosmetics (common/rare)
- Stash expansions

**Economy Balance:**
- Average match earnings: 2,000-5,000 credits
- Good match: 8,000-15,000 credits
- Great match: 20,000+ credits

---

#### 2. Tokens (Premium Currency)

**How to Obtain:**
- Purchase with real money
- Battle Pass rewards (limited)
- Very rare in-game drops
- Events (limited)

**What to Buy:**
- Epic/Legendary cosmetics
- Battle Pass
- Stash expansions (also buyable with credits)
- Operator unlocks (shortcut)
- XP boosters (time-based, not power)
- Unique skins

**NOT for Sale:**
- Weapons (gameplay advantage)
- Armor (gameplay advantage)
- Stat boosts
- Win-rate improvements

---

#### 3. Reputation (Faction Currency)

**Four Factions:**
1. Salvage Corps
2. Tech Syndicate
3. Underground Network
4. Peacekeepers

**How to Earn:**
- Complete faction quests
- Extract faction-specific items
- Daily missions per faction

**What to Buy:**
- Faction-exclusive cosmetics
- Faction weapons (sidegrades, not upgrades)
- Faction lore items
- Special quests

**Reputation Levels:**
```
Neutral:    0 reputation
Friendly:   1,000 reputation
Honored:    5,000 reputation
Revered:    15,000 reputation
Exalted:    50,000 reputation
```

**Faction Choice:**
- Not locked to one faction
- Can level all simultaneously
- Some faction quests conflict (choose wisely)

---

### Marketplace Economy

**Player-to-Player Trading:** (Post-Launch Feature)
- Trade items with other players
- Auction house
- Set buy/sell orders
- Transaction fees (10% credits)

**Market Pricing:**
- Dynamic pricing (supply/demand)
- Price floors (prevent hyperinflation)
- Anti-RMT measures (trading restrictions)

## Quest System

### Quest Types

#### 1. Daily Quests (Reset: 24 hours)

**Quantity:** 3 quests per day

**Examples:**
- "Extract $5,000 worth of loot" → Reward: 2,000 Credits, 500 XP
- "Get 5 kills with Assault Rifles" → Reward: 1,500 Credits, 300 XP
- "Complete 3 matches" → Reward: 1,000 Credits, 200 XP

**Design Goals:**
- Quick to complete (1-3 matches)
- Encourage daily login
- Variety in objectives

---

#### 2. Weekly Quests (Reset: 7 days)

**Quantity:** 5 quests per week

**Examples:**
- "Extract from all 4 extraction zones" → Reward: 10,000 Credits, 2,000 XP
- "Deal 10,000 damage" → Reward: 8,000 Credits, 1,500 XP
- "Extract 10 rare or higher items" → Reward: Rare loot key, 2,000 XP

**Design Goals:**
- More challenging
- Require multiple sessions
- Better rewards

---

#### 3. Story Quests (One-time)

**Progression:**
- Unlock lore
- Unlock new content
- Introduce mechanics

**Example Quest Chain:**

**Quest 1:** "First Steps"
- Objective: Complete tutorial
- Reward: 5,000 Credits, Basic loadout

**Quest 2:** "Into the Fray"
- Objective: Extract successfully 5 times
- Reward: 10,000 Credits, Unlock Recon operator

**Quest 3:** "Faction Allegiance"
- Objective: Choose first faction, complete faction quest
- Reward: 15,000 Credits, Faction cosmetic

**Quest 10:** "The Truth"
- Objective: Find hidden lore items across all maps
- Reward: 50,000 Credits, Legendary item, Lore unlock

---

#### 4. Seasonal Quests

**Duration:** Entire season (2-3 months)

**Objectives:**
- Long-term goals
- Exclusive rewards
- Seasonal cosmetics
- Epic/Legendary items

**Examples:**
- "Extract 1,000,000 Credits worth of loot"
- "Reach Level 60"
- "Win 100 matches"
- "Max out one operator"

---

### Quest Rewards Structure

**Credits:**
- Daily: 1,000-2,000
- Weekly: 5,000-15,000
- Story: 5,000-50,000
- Seasonal: 50,000-100,000

**XP:**
- Daily: 200-500
- Weekly: 1,000-3,000
- Story: Variable
- Seasonal: 10,000-50,000

**Items:**
- Weapon skins
- Operator skins
- Loot keys
- Consumables

## Battle Pass

### Structure

**Tiers:** 100 levels  
**Cost:** $9.99 USD (or 1,000 Tokens)  
**Duration:** 60-90 days per season

**Two Tracks:**
- **Free Track:** Basic rewards (everyone)
- **Premium Track:** Enhanced rewards (paid)

---

### Progression

**XP to Level:**
- Tier 1→2: 1,000 XP
- Consistent: 1,000 XP per tier
- Total to 100: 100,000 XP

**XP Sources:**
- Same as account XP
- Battle Pass challenges (bonus XP)
- Weekly bonus XP (first 10 tiers faster)

**Estimated Time:** 60-80 hours over season

---

### Rewards Example

| Tier | Free Track           | Premium Track                          |
| ---- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| 1    | 500 Credits          | 1,000 Credits                          |
| 5    | Weapon Skin (Common) | Weapon Skin (Rare)                     |
| 10   | -                    | Operator Skin (Rare)                   |
| 15   | 1,000 Credits        | 2,000 Credits + 100 Tokens             |
| 25   | Emote                | Epic Weapon Skin                       |
| 50   | -                    | Epic Operator Skin                     |
| 75   | Rare Skin            | Legendary Weapon Skin                  |
| 100  | Epic Skin            | Legendary Operator Skin + 1,000 Tokens |

**Total Value:**
- Free Track: ~$10 USD equivalent
- Premium Track: ~$80 USD equivalent
- Premium also returns 1,300 Tokens (cost: 1,000)

---

### Battle Pass Features

**Challenges:**
- Daily BP challenges (bonus XP)
- Weekly BP challenges (large XP)
- Tier skips (can purchase with Tokens)

**Catch-up Mechanisms:**
- XP boosters later in season
- Weekend double XP events
- Last week: Increased XP rates

## Monetization Strategy

### Business Model: Free-to-Play

**Core Principle:** NEVER Pay-to-Win

**What's Free:**
- Full game access
- All operators (unlockable)
- All weapons (earnable)
- All maps
- Ranked mode
- Core cosmetics

**What's Paid:**
- Premium cosmetics (skins, emotes)
- Battle Pass
- Convenience (stash space, loadout slots)
- Time savers (XP boosters - NOT power)

---

### Store Structure

#### Featured Section

**Weekly Rotation:**
- 3-5 featured items
- Limited-time bundles
- Discounts (10-30% off)
- New releases

---

#### Cosmetics Shop

**Operator Skins:**
- Common: 500 Credits
- Rare: 1,000 Credits or 100 Tokens
- Epic: 1,500 Credits or 200 Tokens
- Legendary: Tokens only (500-1,000)

**Weapon Skins:**
- Common: 300 Credits
- Rare: 600 Credits or 75 Tokens
- Epic: 1,200 Credits or 150 Tokens
- Legendary: Tokens only (300-600)

**Emotes:**
- Common: 200 Credits
- Rare: 400 Credits or 50 Tokens
- Epic: 800 Credits or 100 Tokens

**Bundles:**
- Operator + matching weapon skins: 20% discount
- Full faction theme sets: 25% discount
- Seasonal bundles: 30% discount

---

#### Convenience Items

**Stash Expansions:**
- +25 slots: 2,000 Credits or 200 Tokens

**Loadout Slots:**
- +1 slot: 1,000 Credits or 100 Tokens

**XP Boosters:**
- 1 hour: 100 Tokens

**Name Change:**
- 1,000 Credits or 100 Tokens

## Conversion & Monetization Goals

### Target Metrics

**Conversion Rate:** 3-5% (free to paying)  
**ARPU:** $2-4 per month  
**ARPPU:** $15-25 per month  
**LTV (6 months):** $40-60  

**Payer Segments:**
- Minnows (57% of payers): $5-20 total
- Dolphins (30%): $20-100 total
- Whales (13%): $100+ total

---

### Fair Monetization Principles

**Transparency:**
- Clear pricing
- No lootboxes (removed due to regulations)
- No gambling mechanics
- Exact item previews

**Value:**
- Battle Pass positive value (return tokens)
- Bundles offer discounts
- Free path viable
- No FOMO pressure (items return to shop)

**No P2W:**
- Cosmetic-only premium items
- No stat boosts for money
- No exclusive powerful weapons
- Fair matchmaking (MMR-based)

---

### Anti-Frustration Features

**No Energy System:**
- Play unlimited matches
- No wait timers
- No "lives" system

**No Forced Ads:**
- Optional rewarded ads (watch for bonus credits)
- Not required for progression

**Reasonable Grind:**
- Earnable through play
- Multiple progression paths
- Catch-up mechanics for new players
