    📋 Quest System Overview#

    Quest Categories#

    Category Duration Complexity Rewards
    Main Story Multi-session High Major unlocks
    Faction Quests 1-3 sessions Medium Reputation + Items
    Daily Quests Single session Low Credits + XP
    Weekly Quests Multi-session Medium Premium rewards
    Challenge Quests Varies Very High Unique items

    🎯 Main Story Quests#

    Chapter 1: “The Newcomer” (Tutorial Arc)#

    Quest 1.1: First Steps

    Giver: System Tutorial
Objective: Complete extraction with any loot
Rewards: 500 Credits, Basic Loadout
Duration: 1 match

    Quest 1.2: Meet the Factions

    Giver: Safe Zone NPC
Objective: Talk to all 4 faction representatives
Rewards: 250 Rep each faction, Map Intel
Duration: Safe zone only

    Quest 1.3: Prove Your Worth

    Giver: Viktor Koval (Salvage Corps)
Objective: Extract with 3 Industrial Materials
Rewards: 1000 Credits, Salvage Rep +500
Duration: 1-2 matches

    Chapter 2: “Whispers of Collapse” (Lore Arc)#

    Quest 2.1: The First Clue

    Giver: Dr. Huang (Tech Syndicate)
Objective: Retrieve Data Drive from Tech Labs
Map: Industrial Decay
Location: Lab Alpha, 3rd Floor
Enemies: 5-7 AI guards
Rewards: 2000 Credits, Tech Rep +750, Audio Log #1

    Quest 2.2: Survivor’s Account

    Giver: Madame X (Underground)
Objective: Find and extract the "Witness" (NPC rescue)
Map: Industrial Decay > Urban Ruins
Stakes: Witness carries key information
Rewards: 3000 Credits, Underground Rep +1000, Lore unlock

    🏴 Faction Quest Templates#

    Salvage Corps Quest Types#

    Type A: Resource Gathering

    Template: "Collect [X] of [Material Type]"
Difficulty: Easy-Medium
Time: 1-2 matches

Example: "Steel Shortage"
- Collect 10 Metal Scraps
- Bonus: Find High-Grade Steel (+500 Rep)
- Reward: 1500 Credits, Worker's Toolbox

    Type B: Repair Missions

    Template: "Repair/Activate [Object] at [Location]"
Difficulty: Medium
Time: 1 match

Example: "Power Restored"
- Reach Generator Room (Reactor F1)
- Repair Generator (30 second interaction)
- Extract successfully
- Reward: 2000 Credits, Reputation +800

    Type C: Convoy Protection

    Template: "Escort [NPC/Object] to [Extraction]"
Difficulty: Hard
Time: Full match

Example: "Supply Run"
- Meet supply cart at Loading Docks
- Escort to Gate 03 (NPC takes damage)
- Protect from 3 AI ambush waves
- Reward: 4000 Credits, Reputation +1200, Rare Item

    Tech Syndicate Quest Types#

    Type A: Data Recovery

    Template: "Retrieve [Data Object] from [Secure Location]"
Difficulty: Medium
Time: 1 match

Example: "Project Prometheus Files"
- Locate Server Room in Lab Beta
- Hack terminal (15 seconds, creates noise)
- Download complete, extract with drive
- Reward: 2000 Credits, Intel item, Rep +750

    Type B: Hack Missions

    Template: "Hack [System] without being detected"
Difficulty: Hard (stealth optional)
Time: 1 match

Example: "Blind Eyes"
- Disable 3 Security Cameras in Offices
- Remain undetected (bonus objective)
- Reward: 2500 Credits, Stealth bonus +500

    Underground Network Quest Types#

    Type A: Delivery/Smuggling

    Template: "Transport [Package] to [Dead Drop]"
Difficulty: Medium
Time: 1 match

Example: "Hot Goods"
- Pick up package at Warehouse W2 (marked)
- Deliver to Dead Drop in Sewers
- Avoid Peacekeepers patrol (optional)
- Reward: 3000 Credits, Black Market access

    Type B: Elimination

    Template: "Eliminate [Target] in [Zone]"
Difficulty: Hard
Time: 1 match

Example: "No Witnesses"
- Kill named AI target "The Informant"
- Location: Office Complex, Floor 4
- Target has bodyguards (3 AI)
- Bonus: Retrieve documents from body
- Reward: 5000 Credits, Rep +1500

    Peacekeepers Quest Types#

    Type A: Zone Clearing

    Template: "Eliminate [X] hostiles in [Zone]"
Difficulty: Medium
Time: 1 match

Example: "Clean Sweep"
- Kill 15 Scav AI in Warehouse District
- Bonus: No civilian casualties
- Reward: 2000 Credits, Ammo crate

    Type B: Rescue Operations

    Template: "Rescue [Survivor] from [Danger]"
Difficulty: Hard (timed)
Time: 1 match, time limit

Example: "Trapped"
- Locate survivor in collapsed building
- Clear 5 enemies guarding area
- Escort survivor to Convoy extraction
- Time limit: 8 minutes
- Reward: 4000 Credits, Medical supplies, Rep +1200

    📅 Daily Quest System#

    Daily Quest Pool (Rotating)#

    Easy Tier (Pick 1):

    Quest Objective Reward
    Loot Run Extract with 5+ items 500 Credits
    Scavenger Loot 10 containers 400 Credits
    Survivor Extract 2 matches 600 Credits

    Medium Tier (Pick 1):

    Quest Objective Reward
    Combat Expert Kill 10 AI enemies 800 Credits
    Valuable Find Extract with Rare+ item 1000 Credits
    Hot Zone Hero Loot from Hot Zone 900 Credits

    Hard Tier (Pick 1):

    Quest Objective Reward
    Untouchable Extract without taking damage 1500 Credits
    Boss Hunter Kill any AI Boss 2000 Credits
    Full Bag Extract with full inventory 1200 Credits

    Daily Reset#

    • Resets at: 00:00 UTC
    • Cannot stack uncompleted quests
    • Completed quests give bonus streak

    📆 Weekly Quest System#

    Weekly Challenge Format#

    Week Example: “Industrial Dominance”

    Day Objective Individual Reward
    Mon Extract 3 times from Industrial Decay 1000 Credits
    Tue Kill 25 AI in Industrial Decay 1200 Credits
    Wed Loot the Reactor Core 1500 Credits
    Thu Complete 1 Faction Quest 2000 Credits
    Fri Defeat Reactor Guardian Boss 2500 Credits
    Sat-Sun Complete ALL objectives BONUS: Rare Weapon

    Completion Bonus:

    • 5/5 Days = Rare Weapon Crate
    • 7/7 Days = Epic Cosmetic

    🏆 Challenge Quests (Special)#

    Operator Personal Quests#

    Example: Ghost’s Quest Chain “Ghosts of Unit Seven”

    Part 1: “Dog Tags”

    Unlock: Level 3 with Ghost operator
Objective: Find Unit Seven memorial in Office Complex
Location: Floor 5, locked room (keycard)
Reward: Audio Log, Ghost backstory, 2000 Credits

    Part 2: “One Survivor”

    Unlock: Complete Part 1
Objective: Retrieve encrypted comm device from Tech Labs
Location: Lab Beta, Server Room
Challenge: Stealth recommended, alarms alert extra AI
Reward: Lore reveal, Quest item, 3000 Credits

    Part 3: “The Rendezvous”

    Unlock: Complete Part 2
Objective: Extract from Helipad at specific time (10:00-11:00)
Challenge: High-traffic time, PvP likely
Trigger: NPC contact appears with information
Reward: Major lore, Ghost exclusive skin unlock progress

    Part 4: “Traitor’s End”

    Unlock: Complete Part 3
Objective: Eliminate special AI target "The Betrayer"
Location: Rotating spawn
Challenge: Boss-tier enemy with unique patterns
Reward: Story conclusion, "Memorial" skin, 10,000 Credits

    📊 Quest Balance Guidelines#

    Reward Scaling#

    Difficulty Time Est. Credit Reward Rep Reward
    Easy 5-10 min 500-1000 200-400
    Medium 10-20 min 1000-2500 500-800
    Hard 20-30 min 2500-5000 800-1200
    Extreme 30+ min 5000-10000 1200-2000

    Quest Availability Rules#

    • Max 3 Daily quests active
    • Max 1 Weekly quest chain
    • Faction quests: Unlimited (gated by reputation)
    • Main Story: Sequential unlock
