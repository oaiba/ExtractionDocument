# Resource Library

## UI/UX Design
- **Game UI Database**: [https://www.gameuidatabase.com/index.php](https://www.gameuidatabase.com/index.php) - Comprehensive reference for game UI/UX patterns and screens. Use this for inspiration and reference when designing menus, HUDs, and inventory systems.

## Game Design & Mechanics
- **Gamasutra (Game Developer)**: [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design) - In-depth articles on game design theory and practice.
- **The Art of Game Design (Jesse Schell)**: Classic resource for fundamental design principles (look for the "Book of Lenses").
- **Extraction Shooter Core Loop**: Analyze breakdowns of *Escape from Tarkov* and *Hunt: Showdown* on YouTube (e.g., GDC talks, design analysis channels like Game Maker's Toolkit).
- **FPS Design Patterns**: [https://www.gamedesigning.org/learn/fps-game-design/](https://www.gamedesigning.org/learn/fps-game-design/) - Overview of FPS mechanics and level design.

## Economy & Progression
- **Machinations.io**: [https://machinations.io/](https://machinations.io/) - Tool and resources for modeling game economies and systems. essential for balancing risk/reward in extraction shooters.
- **Deconstructor of Fun**: [https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/) - Deep dives into F2P and game economy models.

## Level Design
- **Level Design Book**: [https://book.leveldesign.io/](https://book.leveldesign.io/) - Comprehensive guide to level design theory and practice.
- **World of Level Design**: [https://www.worldofleveldesign.com/](https://www.worldofleveldesign.com/) - Tutorials and articles focused on level design, often with Unreal Engine examples.

## Audio Design
- **Sonniss GDC Bundles**: [https://sonniss.com/gameaudio-gdc-bundle-archive](https://sonniss.com/gameaudio-gdc-bundle-archive) - Massive archive of free, high-quality sound effects released annually for GDC. Great for placeholder and production assets.
- **GDC Vault - Audio**: [https://gdcvault.com/browse/track/Audio](https://gdcvault.com/browse/track/Audio) - Talks on sound design from industry professionals (look for FPS/Tactical audio talks).

## Unreal Engine Resources
- **Unreal Engine Documentation**: [https://docs.unrealengine.com/5.0/en-US/](https://docs.unrealengine.com/5.0/en-US/) - The official manual.
- **Epic Developer Community**: [https://dev.epicgames.com/community/learning](https://dev.epicgames.com/community/learning) - Tutorials and courses directly from Epic Games.
- **Lyra Starter Game**: [https://docs.unrealengine.com/5.0/en-US/lyra-sample-game-in-unreal-engine/](https://docs.unrealengine.com/5.0/en-US/lyra-sample-game-in-unreal-engine/) - Essential study material for modern UE5 multiplayer shooter architecture.
