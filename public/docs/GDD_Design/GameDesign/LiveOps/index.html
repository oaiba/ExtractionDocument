    Live Operations & Events#

    ← Back to Index | Next: Overview →

    🎯 Live Ops Philosophy#

    Core Principle: “Always a reason to play”

    Live Operations should:

    • Keep the game fresh - New content regularly
    • Reward engagement - Active players benefit more
    • Create FOMO responsibly - Urgency without exploitation
    • Build community - Shared experiences and events
    • Drive revenue - Without being predatory

    📅 Content Calendar Structure#

    Annual Overview#

    Year 1 Structure:
├── Season 1: "First Contact" (Launch + 90 days)
├── Season 2: "Industrial Expansion" (Days 91-180)
├── Season 3: "Urban Decay" (Days 181-270)
└── Season 4: "Military Reckoning" (Days 271-365)

Each Season Contains:
├── 1 Battle Pass (90 days)
├── 1 Major Content Drop (New map/operators)
├── 4-6 Events (2-3 weeks each)
├── 12 Weekly Rotations
└── Daily Challenges

    Monthly Cadence#

    Week Content Notes
    Week 1 Major Update Patch notes, new content
    Week 2 Event Launch 2-week event begins
    Week 3 Mid-Season Sale Cosmetic discounts
    Week 4 Event End + Teaser Wrap-up, next event preview

    Weekly Cadence#

    Day Activity
    Monday Weekly challenges reset
    Tuesday Maintenance window (2 AM - 4 AM)
    Wednesday Mid-week update (if needed)
    Thursday New shop rotation
    Friday Weekend event begins
    Saturday-Sunday Peak playtime, events active

    🎪 Event Types#

    1. Double XP/Credits Weekends#

    Frequency: Every 2-4 weeks Duration: Friday 6 PM → Monday 6 AM (local)

    Types:

    Event Bonus
    Double XP Weekend +100% account XP
    Double Credits Weekend +100% credit earning
    Double Battle Pass Weekend +100% BP XP
    Double Operator Weekend +100% operator XP

    Design Notes:

    • Easy to implement
    • High engagement driver
    • Great for new player retention

    2. Limited-Time Game Modes#

    Frequency: Every 4-6 weeks Duration: 1-2 weeks

    Night Ops Mode#

    Theme: All matches at night
Visibility: Reduced (flashlights required)
Changes: Louder footsteps, suppressor advantage
Rewards: Unique night camo skin
Duration: 2 weeks

    Chaos Mode#

    Theme: Fast-paced action
Changes: 2x loot spawns, 3x AI spawns, 10-minute matches
Rewards: High-octane weapon charm
Duration: 1 week

    Extraction Rush#

    Theme: Speed extraction
Changes: Extra extraction points, faster extraction timer
Rewards: Speed-themed cosmetics
Duration: 1 week

    Hardcore Mode#

    Theme: Realistic difficulty
Changes: No HUD, 2x damage, no respawn, friendly fire
Rewards: Hardcore veteran badge
Duration: 2 weeks

    Solo Showdown#

    Theme: No squads allowed
Changes: Solo-only queue, adjusted AI
Rewards: Lone wolf skin
Duration: 1 week

    3. Collection Events#

    Frequency: Once per season Duration: 3-4 weeks

    Structure:

    Event Currency: "Fragments" (earned per match)
Collection: 24 event-exclusive items
Tiers: 
  - Tier 1 (0-50 Frags): Common items
  - Tier 2 (50-150 Frags): Uncommon items
  - Tier 3 (150-300 Frags): Rare items
  - Tier 4 (300-500 Frags): Epic items
  - Bonus (500+ Frags): Legendary item

    Earning Fragments:

    Activity Fragments
    Match completion 5
    Extraction +10
    Event challenges 10-50
    Purchase (optional) 100 for $5

    4. Faction Wars#

    Frequency: Twice per season Duration: 2 weeks

    Mechanics:

    Players choose a faction at event start
All matches contribute to faction score
Global leaderboard tracks faction progress
Winning faction: Bonus rewards for all members
Personal contribution: Individual leaderboard

    Rewards:

    Placement Reward
    Winning Faction (all) 5,000 Credits + Badge
    Top 10% Contributors Exclusive skin
    Top 1% Contributors Legendary item

    5. Boss Hunt Events#

    Frequency: Once per season (new boss) Duration: 2-3 weeks

    Structure:

    New temporary boss spawns on map
Community goal: Kill boss 1,000,000 times globally
Progress tracked publicly
Milestones unlock rewards for everyone
Final milestone: Permanent boss addition

    Milestones Example:

    Kills Reward
    100,000 +10% Credits (all players)
    250,000 Rare weapon skin
    500,000 Epic armor skin
    750,000 Boss taunt emote
    1,000,000 Legendary boss-themed weapon

    6. Holiday Events#

    Annual Calendar:

    Holiday Timing Theme Duration
    Lunar New Year January/February Eastern theme, red/gold 2 weeks
    Valentine’s February 14 Team-focused, duo bonuses 1 week
    Spring Festival March/April Renewal, bright colors 2 weeks
    Summer Bash June-August Beach, tropical 4 weeks
    Halloween October Horror, spooky 3 weeks
    Winter Holiday December Snow, festive 4 weeks

    Holiday Content:

    • Limited-time cosmetics
    • Themed map variations
    • Special game modes
    • Free gifts (login rewards)

    💰 Monetization Events#

    Flash Sales#

    Frequency: Weekly (Thursdays) Duration: 48-72 hours

    Types:

    Sale Type Discount
    Daily Deal 30-50% off single item
    Bundle Special 25% off curated bundle
    Token Sale Bonus tokens with purchase
    Returning Player Extra discount for lapsed players

    Battle Pass#

    Structure (Each Season):

    Tiers: 100 levels
Cost: 1,000 Tokens ($9.99)
Free Track: ~$10 value
Premium Track: ~$80+ value

Features:
- Instant unlock items at purchase
- Tier skip purchase option
- Bonus challenges for faster progress
- Premium returns 1,300 Tokens (profit if completed)

    Limited Bundles#

    Frequency: 2-3 per month Duration: 1 week availability

    Bundle Types:

    Type Contents Price Range
    Operator Bundle Operator skin + weapon skin + charm $15-25
    Weapon Bundle 3 weapon skins + charm $10-15
    Ultimate Bundle Multiple operators + weapons + extras $30-50
    Starter Bundle Value bundle for new players $5-10

    📢 Communication Strategy#

    Announcement Channels#

    Channel Content Frequency
    In-Game News Events, updates, maintenance Daily
    Push Notifications Events starting, rewards expiring 2-3/week
    Social Media Teasers, community content, patch notes Daily
    Email Newsletter, returning player offers Weekly
    Discord Community engagement, feedback Ongoing

    Teaser Timeline#

    For Major Content:

    4 weeks before: Mysterious teaser (silhouette, audio)
3 weeks before: First reveal (character art, name)
2 weeks before: Gameplay preview
1 week before: Full details, abilities, stats
Launch day: In-game trailer, content live

    📊 Live Ops Metrics#

    Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)#

    Engagement:

    Metric Target Red Flag
    DAU (Daily Active Users) Growth or stable -10% week-over-week
    Session Length 25-35 min Below 15 min
    Sessions per Day 2-3 Below 1.5
    D1 Retention 40%+ Below 30%
    D7 Retention 20%+ Below 15%
    D30 Retention 10%+ Below 5%

    Monetization:

    Metric Target Notes
    ARPDAU $0.10-0.30 Average revenue per daily active user
    Conversion Rate 3-5% Free to paid players
    Battle Pass Purchase 15-25% of active players Per season
    Whale % 1-2% Players spending $100+/month

    Event-Specific:

    Metric Target
    Event Participation 60%+ of DAU
    Event Completion 30%+ of participants
    Event Revenue Lift +20% during events

    🔄 Content Pipeline#

    Content Development Cycle#

    T-12 weeks: Concept & Design
T-8 weeks: Production begins
T-4 weeks: Internal testing
T-2 weeks: QA & Polish
T-1 week: Staging deployment
T-0: Live deployment
T+1 week: Monitoring & hotfixes
T+4 weeks: Post-mortem analysis

    Content Types & Lead Times#

    Content Lead Time Team Size
    New Operator 8-12 weeks 8-10 people
    New Map 16-24 weeks 15-20 people
    New Weapon 4-6 weeks 3-4 people
    Cosmetic Bundle 2-4 weeks 2-3 people
    Game Mode 4-8 weeks 4-6 people
    Event 4-6 weeks 3-5 people
    Balance Patch 1-2 weeks 2-3 people

    🛡️ Player Protection#

    Anti-FOMO Measures#

    Measure Description
    Event Reruns Major events return annually
    Archive Shop Past event items available (higher price)
    Catch-Up Mechanics Accelerated BP progress late in season
    Clear Communication Always show when items are leaving
    No Pay-to-Win All purchasable items are cosmetic

    Spending Limits#

    Limit Value
    Daily Transaction Limit $100
    Monthly Spending Warning $200 (opt-in alert)
    Parental Controls Purchase passwords, limits
    Refund Window 72 hours (unused items)

    🛠️ Incident Response#

    Live Issues Protocol#

    Severity Levels:

    Level Description Response Time
    P0 Game unplayable 30 minutes
    P1 Major feature broken 2 hours
    P2 Minor feature broken 24 hours
    P3 Cosmetic/visual bug Next patch

    Communication Template:

    [STATUS] Issue Identified
We're aware of [ISSUE DESCRIPTION] affecting [PLAYERS AFFECTED].
Our team is investigating. Updates will follow.
ETA: [TIME ESTIMATE]

[RESOLVED] Issue Fixed
[ISSUE] has been resolved as of [TIME].
[COMPENSATION if applicable]
Thank you for your patience.

    Compensation Guidelines#

    Issue Duration Compensation
    < 1 hour Nothing or small credit gift
    1-4 hours 1,000 Credits
    4-12 hours 2,500 Credits + XP boost
    12-24 hours 5,000 Credits + event extension
    Major data loss Case-by-case (full restoration)
