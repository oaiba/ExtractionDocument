    Combat Philosophy

    Core Principles:

    1. Tactical Over Twitch - Positioning and decision-making matter more than raw reflexes
    2. Weapon Variety - Each weapon has distinct role and feel
    3. Risk vs Reward - Better weapons come with higher loss penalty
    4. Skill Expression - High skill ceiling with recoil control and positioning
    5. Mobile Optimized - Controls and mechanics designed for touch screens

    Weapon Categories

    Primary Weapons

    1. Assault Rifles (AR)

    Role: Versatile all-rounders, effective at medium range

    Characteristics:

    • Damage: 30-40 per shot
    • Fire Rate: 600-750 RPM
    • Effective Range: 30-50 meters
    • Magazine Size: 30 rounds
    • Recoil: Moderate, controllable

    Weapon Examples:

    AK-47 (Common)

    • Damage: 35
    • Fire Rate: 600 RPM
    • Recoil: High vertical
    • Special: High damage, cheap cost
    • Best for: Budget runs, new players

    M4A1 (Uncommon)

    • Damage: 32
    • Fire Rate: 750 RPM
    • Recoil: Low, very controllable
    • Special: Versatile, reliable
    • Best for: All situations

    SCAR-H (Rare)

    • Damage: 40
    • Fire Rate: 625 RPM
    • Recoil: Moderate
    • Special: High damage per shot
    • Best for: Mid-long range engagements

    Tactical Considerations:

    • Strong opening weapon
    • Good for holding angles
    • Versatile in most combat scenarios
    • Magazine management important

    2. Submachine Guns (SMG)

    Role: Close-quarters specialist, high mobility

    Characteristics:

    • Damage: 20-28 per shot
    • Fire Rate: 900-1100 RPM
    • Effective Range: 10-25 meters
    • Magazine Size: 25-35 rounds
    • Recoil: High but manageable

    Weapon Examples:

    MP5 (Common)

    • Damage: 24
    • Fire Rate: 900 RPM
    • Recoil: Moderate
    • Special: Reliable CQB weapon
    • Best for: Indoor combat

    Vector .45 (Rare)

    • Damage: 28
    • Fire Rate: 1100 RPM
    • Recoil: Very low (unique recoil system)
    • Special: Extremely fast TTK close range
    • Best for: Aggressive pushes

    P90 (Uncommon)

    • Damage: 22
    • Fire Rate: 1000 RPM
    • Magazine: 50 rounds
    • Special: Large magazine, suppressive fire
    • Best for: Squad suppression

    Tactical Considerations:

    • Excellent for building clearing
    • High movement speed
    • Weak at range
    • Ammo consumption high

    3. Shotguns

    Role: Point-blank devastation, room clearing

    Characteristics:

    • Damage: 15-25 per pellet (8 pellets)
    • Fire Rate: 60-150 RPM (pump vs auto)
    • Effective Range: 5-15 meters
    • Magazine/Tube: 4-8 shells
    • Recoil: Very high

    Weapon Examples:

    Remington 870 (Common)

    • Damage: 20 per pellet x 8
    • Fire Rate: 60 RPM (pump action)
    • Special: One-shot potential close range
    • Best for: Ambush, close defense

    AA-12 (Epic)

    • Damage: 18 per pellet x 8
    • Fire Rate: 300 RPM (full-auto)
    • Special: Automatic shotgun
    • Best for: Room clearing, CQB dominance

    Tactical Considerations:

    • Devastating in confined spaces
    • Useless at medium-long range
    • Limited ammo capacity
    • High risk/high reward weapon choice

    4. Sniper Rifles

    Role: Long-range elimination, overwatch

    Characteristics:

    • Damage: 75-120 per shot
    • Fire Rate: 40-60 RPM (bolt action)
    • Effective Range: 50-100+ meters
    • Magazine Size: 5-10 rounds
    • Recoil: Very high

    Weapon Examples:

    M24 (Rare)

    • Damage: 85
    • Fire Rate: 50 RPM
    • Special: Reliable bolt-action
    • Best for: Overwatch positions

    AWP (Epic)

    • Damage: 120
    • Fire Rate: 40 RPM
    • Special: One-shot to chest (no armor)
    • Best for: High-stakes elimination

    SVD (Uncommon - Semi-Auto)

    • Damage: 75
    • Fire Rate: 180 RPM
    • Special: Semi-automatic, faster follow-up
    • Best for: Aggressive sniping

    Tactical Considerations:

    • Requires positioning and patience
    • Top-down perspective makes sniping unique
    • Best used with high ground advantage
    • Vulnerable if rushed

    5. Light Machine Guns (LMG)

    Role: Suppression, sustained fire

    Characteristics:

    • Damage: 30-38 per shot
    • Fire Rate: 600-800 RPM
    • Effective Range: 30-60 meters
    • Magazine Size: 75-100 rounds
    • Recoil: High initially, settles

    Weapon Examples:

    M249 SAW (Rare)

    • Damage: 32
    • Fire Rate: 750 RPM
    • Magazine: 100 rounds
    • Special: Bipod for reduced recoil
    • Best for: Area denial, suppression

    PKM (Uncommon)

    • Damage: 38
    • Fire Rate: 650 RPM
    • Magazine: 75 rounds
    • Special: High damage, heavy
    • Best for: Defensive positions

    Tactical Considerations:

    • Heavy weapon (reduced movement speed)
    • Excellent for holding choke points
    • Long reload times (vulnerability)
    • Suppresses enemy movement effectively

    Ammunition

    Ammo System Overview

    Philosophy: Ammunition is the bridge between the weapon and the target. While the gun dictates handling, the Ammo Type dictates the result of the impact. The game features a realistic caliber system where different weapons share ammo pools, creating strategic inventory management choices.

    Ammo Tiers & Variants: Each caliber has multiple variants, creating a “rock-paper-scissors” dynamic against armor.

    1. Standard (FMJ/Ball)

      • Role: Balanced Performance
      • Pros: Cheap, reliable, moderate flesh damage, moderate penetration.
      • Cons: Struggles against Level 4+ Armor.

    2. Armor Piercing (AP)

      • Role: Armor Destroyer
      • Pros: High penetration (ignores armor), higher velocity.
      • Cons: Reduced flesh damage (over-penetration), rare/expensive.
      • Use Case: Late-game PvP, Boss hunting.

    3. Hollow Point (HP/RIP)

      • Role: Flesh Tearer
      • Pros: Massive flesh damage, causes heavy bleeding.
      • Cons: Very low penetration (stopped by Level 1-2 armor).
      • Use Case: “Leg Meta” (shooting unarmored limbs), hunting mutants/AI.

    4. Tracer

      • Role: Visual Feedback
      • Pros: Visible flight path (helps aiming), usually mixed stats.
      • Cons: Reveals shooter position.

    Weapon Modifications

    Attachment System

    Optics:

    • Red Dot Sight: +Accuracy, basic zoom
    • Holographic Sight: +Precision
    • 4x Scope: +Long range effectiveness
    • Thermal Scope (Epic): See through smoke

    Barrel:

    • Suppressor: -Sound, -Range slightly
    • Compensator: -Recoil
    • Extended Barrel: +Range, +Recoil

    Stock:

    • Tactical Stock: +Aim stability
    • Light Stock: +Movement speed
    • Heavy Stock: -Recoil, -Speed

    Magazine:

    • Extended Mag: +Capacity (+10 rounds)
    • Fast Mag: +Reload speed
    • Drum Mag: +Capacity (+20 rounds, -Movement)

    Underbarrel:

    • Foregrip: -Recoil
    • Laser Sight: +Hip-fire accuracy
    • Tactical Light: +Visibility in dark

    Attachment Slots:

    • Common weapons: 2 slots
    • Uncommon: 3 slots
    • Rare: 4 slots
    • Epic/Legendary: 5 slots

    Combat Mechanics

    Damage System

    Damage Model:

    Base Damage → Armor Absorption → Health Damage

    Armor Mechanics:

    • Absorbs 70% of incoming damage
    • Depletes with damage taken
    • Does not regenerate (must replace)
    • Different armor tiers (Light/Medium/Heavy)

    Health:

    • Base: 100 HP
    • Does not auto-regenerate
    • Requires med items to heal
    • Bleeds if hit (slow HP drain)

    Headshot Multipliers:

    • No helmet: 2.0x damage
    • Light helmet (Common): 1.5x damage
    • Medium helmet (Rare): 1.3x damage
    • Heavy helmet (Epic): 1.1x damage

    Limb Modifiers:

    • Head: See above
    • Chest: 1.0x (full damage)
    • Arms: 0.9x
    • Legs: 0.8x

    Recoil System

    Recoil Patterns:

    • Each weapon has unique recoil pattern
    • Vertical recoil (moves crosshair up)
    • Horizontal drift (left/right variation)
    • First-shot accuracy bonus

    Player Control:

    • Manual compensation required
    • Attachments reduce recoil
    • Crouching reduces recoil 20%
    • Burst firing more accurate

    Mobile Considerations:

    • Gyroscope support for recoil control
    • Auto-recoil compensation option (reduced)
    • Haptic feedback for feedback

    Ballistics

    Projectile System:

    • Hitscan at close range (<20m)
    • Projectile travel at long range (>20m)
    • Bullet drop (minimal for gameplay)
    • Penetration system

    Penetration:

    • Wood: Full penetration, 20% damage loss
    • Thin Metal: 50% penetration chance, 40% damage loss
    • Concrete: No penetration
    • Players: Over-penetration (multiple enemies)

    Cover System

    Soft Cover:

    • Wood crates, thin walls
    • Provides concealment
    • Can be penetrated
    • Breakable (destructible)

    Hard Cover:

    • Concrete walls, vehicles, metal
    • Provides protection
    • Cannot be penetrated
    • Permanent

    Mechanics:

    • Auto-crouch near cover (optional)
    • Peek left/right buttons
    • Blind fire option (reduced accuracy)

    Throwables & Equipment

    • Damage: 100 (direct), 50-10 (falloff)
    • Radius: 8 meters
    • Fuse: 3 seconds
    • Cookable: Yes (hold before throw)

    • Radius: 10 meters
    • No damage

    • Duration: 15 seconds
    • Radius: 8 meters
    • Blocks vision (not thermal)

    EMP Grenade (Specialist)

    • Disables abilities 10 seconds
    • Destroys deployables
    • Radius: 15 meters

    Medical Items

    Bandage (Common)

    • Heals: 15 HP over 3 seconds
    • Stops bleeding
    • Use time: 3 seconds

    Medkit (Uncommon)

    • Heals: 50 HP over 5 seconds
    • Use time: 5 seconds

    Combat Stim (Rare)

    • Heals: 30 HP instantly
    • +10% speed for 10 sec
    • Use time: 1 second

    Surgery Kit (Epic)

    • Heals: 100 HP over 8 seconds
    • Removes all negative effects
    • Use time: 8 seconds (vulnerable)

    Combat Scenarios

    Engagement Ranges

    Close (0-15m):

    • Best: SMG, Shotgun
    • Tactics: Movement, hip-fire
    • Cover: Duck in/out quickly

    Medium (15-40m):

    • Best: AR, LMG
    • Tactics: Controlled bursts, positioning
    • Cover: Hold angles, pre-aim

    Long (40m+):

    • Best: Sniper, AR (scoped)
    • Tactics: Patience, first-shot accuracy
    • Cover: Minimize exposure

    Movement During Combat

    Sprint:

    • Cannot shoot
    • Fast repositioning
    • Weapon ready time: 0.5 sec

    Walk:

    • Shoot while moving
    • Reduced accuracy
    • Standard speed

    Crouch:

    • Better accuracy
    • Slower movement
    • Smaller target

    Prone (Future):

    • Best accuracy
    • Very slow movement
    • Hard to spot

    ##Time to Kill (TTK)

    Target: 100 HP, 50 Armor

    Weapon Type TTK (Optimal) Shots to Kill
    SMG 0.2-0.4s 6-8 shots
    AR 0.3-0.5s 5-7 shots
    Shotgun 0.1-0.2s 1-2 shots
    Sniper 0.0s (instant) 1-2 shots
    LMG 0.4-0.6s 6-8 shots
    Pistol 0.5-0.8s 8-10 shots

    Design Goal: TTK fast enough to reward skill, slow enough for counterplay

    Weapon Balance Principles

    Balance Factors:

    1. Availability - Common weapons easier to obtain
    2. Risk - Expensive weapons = more loss on death
    3. Skill Requirement - Higher-tier weapons need more skill
    4. Situational - No “best weapon”, all have roles

    Meta Prevention:

    • Regular balance patches
    • Weapon rotation in loot pools
    • Seasonal adjustments
    • Community feedback integration

    Visual & Audio Feedback

    Visual Effects

    Muzzle Flash:

    • Reveals shooter position
    • Suppressor reduces flash
    • Different per weapon

    • Spark (metal)
    • Dust (concrete)
    • Splinter (wood)
    • Blood spray (hit confirmation)

    Tracer Rounds:

    • Visible bullet trails
    • Every 5th round (balance)
    • Helps with aiming feedback

    Audio Design

    Gunshot Sounds:

    • Distinct per weapon class
    • Volume indicates distance
    • Direction for enemy awareness
    • Suppressed variants quieter

    Reload Sounds:

    • Indicate vulnerability window
    • Distinct click-clack feedback
    • Magazine drop, insertion, chamber
