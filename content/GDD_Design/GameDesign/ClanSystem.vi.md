## Tổng Quan

Clans tạo long-term social identity, shared goals, và group competition mà không forcing every người chơi into organized play.

The clan hệ thống should giúp người chơi find reliable squadmates và build identity across seasons. It không nên make solo play feel invalid, và it không nên become a gear funnel where large groups can manufacture unfair power for members.

Clan giá trị should come from coordination, recognition, và shared progress: banners, missions, event leaderboards, social tools, và light economic convenience với strict limits.

## Clan Lifecycle

The lifecycle gives groups a reason to return together. Creation is a commitment, recruitment builds the roster, missions tạo weekly goals, và seasonal competition gives the clan a public story.

| Step | Action | kết quả |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | tạo clan | Name, tag, leader, và initial member cap are set |
| 2 | Recruit members | Clan gains active contributors |
| 3 | Complete clan missions | Members generate clan XP và event contribution |
| 4 | Earn milestones | Clan unlocks cosmetics, caps, và social identity rewards |
| 5 | Compete in Faction Wars | Clan contribution affects leaderboard và seasonal rewards |
| 6 | Continue mission cycle | Clan has recurring reasons to play together |

## Creation Rules

Creation rules protect the namespace và reduce spam. The chi phí và level yêu cầu nên được high enough to discourage throwaway clans nhưng low enough that motivated friend groups can start early.

| yêu cầu | Direction |
| :--- | :--- |
| Account level | Minimum level to prevent spam |
| Creation chi phí | Credit sink |
| Name rules | Profanity filter và uniqueness |
| Tag rules | Short, dễ đọc, moderated |
| Initial cap | Modest member cap, expandable thông qua levels |

## Roles và Permissions

Roles should reduce administration friction while protecting members from abuse. Any action that removes người chơi, spends shared resources, hoặc changes clan identity needs an audit trail.

| Role | Permissions |
| :--- | :--- |
| Leader | Full control, disband, promote, bank policy |
| Officer | Invite, kick lower ranks, start missions |
| Veteran | Invite và manage mission participation |
| Member | Contribute và receive rewards |
| Recruit | Limited access until trusted |

## Clan Progression

| source | Contribution |
| :--- | :--- |
| Clan missions | primary clan XP |
| Faction Wars | Event contribution và leaderboard points |
| Squad extraction | Small contribution khi clanmates play together |
| Donations | Controlled contribution to clan bank |

## Clan Bank

The clan bank is a cooperation tính năng, not a vũ khí pipeline. It can support event materials, cosmetic goals, và controlled shared resources, nhưng it không được let a few wealthy người chơi mua combat readiness for the whole roster.

| Rule | yêu cầu |
| :--- | :--- |
| Deposits | Credits và event materials only |
| Withdrawals | Role-gated |
| Audit log | Required |
| Power limit | Cannot tạo pay-to-win gear funnel |

## Clan Examples

A casual clan of friends should benefit from easy invites, shared missions, và a hiển thị rõ tag mà không needing to compete on leaderboards. The hệ thống should support low-pressure identity.

A competitive clan should have seasonal goals, Faction War contribution, và leaderboard visibility. Their advantage should come from coordination và activity, not hidden stat bonuses.

A new recruit should have limited permissions until trusted. This protects the bank, roster, và clan identity while still letting the người chơi contribute to missions.

## Clan Failure Cases

- nếu clan rewards tạo combat power, solo và small-group người chơi will feel forced into clans.
- nếu permissions are unclear, leaders và officers can accidentally abuse shared resources.
- nếu missions require full groups only, casual clans will stagnate.
- nếu clan names và tags are not moderated, social spaces degrade quickly.
