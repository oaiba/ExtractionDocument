# Combat, Weapons & Gear

###  The Art of Violence

Combat in **Extraction Shooter** is high-stakes and unforgiving. With **low TTK (Time to Kill)** and permanent loss of gear on death, every engagement is a calculated risk.

{{< callout type="warning" >}} **Golden Rule:** Gear is not just progression—it is a _consumable resource_. Losing a high-tier rifle hurts, but it drives the economy.
{{< /callout >}}

#### Core Mechanics

* **Ballistics:** Realistic bullet drop and travel time.
* **Recoil:** Pattern-based with random deviation.
* **Suppression:** Heavy fire reduces accuracy and blurs vision.
* **Armor System:** Durability-based protection. AP ammo shreds armor; HP ammo shreds flesh.

###  Inventory Management

Combat starts before the raid begins.

* **Weight System:** Heavy gear reduces movement speed and stamina recovery.
* **Grid Inventory:** "Tetris-style" inventory forces tough decisions on what to keep.
* **Hotbar Limits:** Only 2 weapons and 4 utility slots per raid. Choose wisely.

For grid inventory details, secure container rules, and the marketplace, see [Looting & Inventory](https://github.com/oaiba/ExtractionDocument/blob/main/content/GDD_Design/Gameplay/Looting_Interactions/README.md).
