## Role Guidance

### For Design & Art Teams
*   **Single Source of Truth**: Always refer to the [Design Hub](/GDD_Design/) before starting any creative work.
*   **Visual Consistency**: Follow the [Style Guide](/GDD_Design/Visuals/StyleGuide) strictly to ensure cross-platform visual fidelity.
*   **Feedback Loop**: When updating mechanics, ensure the [Core Loop](/GDD_Design/GameDesign/CoreGameplay) is adjusted to reflect the change.
*   **Asset Submission**: Use the technical specs in [Asset Guidelines](/GDD_Design/Visuals/AssetGuidelines) to prepare models for UE5.

### For Technical & Dev Teams
*   **Implementation Specs**: All Enums, Codenames, and Interface contracts are defined in the [Technical Hub](/GDD_Technical/).
*   **Performance First**: Adhere to the budgets defined in the [Performance & Optimization](/GDD_Technical/Performance/Optimization) section.
*   **Task Management**: Follow the [Development Roadmap](/GDD_Technical/Core/DevelopmentRoadmap) for milestone priorities.
*   **Code Standards**: Maintain modularity as outlined in the [Architecture](/GDD_Technical/Core/Architecture) documentation.
