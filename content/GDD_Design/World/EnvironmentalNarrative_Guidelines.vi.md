## The Silent Storyteller

The **Neo-Asia Industrial Corridor (NAIC)** is dead — five years sau The Collapse. There are no functioning governments, no emergency services, no safe zones. The *environment* phải được the primary method of delivering lore — every rusted beam và bloodstained wall tells a story the người chơi discovers, never a story they are told.

### cốt lõi Principle: "The Aftermath"
Every location should look like something *happened* there right trước the người chơi arrived. Avoid static, perfect presets.

## Narrative Layers

### Layer 1: The Panic (Immediate Past)
*Shows the moment society collapsed.*
*   **Asset:** Suitcases spilled open on a highway.
*   **Implication:** People fled in a hurry.
*   **Asset:** Half-eaten meals on a restaurant bảng.
*   **Implication:** The disaster struck instantly, unexpectedly.
*   **Asset:** Cars crashed into barricades, not just parked.
*   **Implication:** Drivers were desperate hoặc terrified.

### Layer 2: The Struggle (Recent Past)
*Shows survivors trying to live in the ruins.*
*   **Asset:** A mattress in a bank vault.
*   **Implication:** Someone thought money would keep them safe, hoặc the thick walls were shelter.
*   **Asset:** Rain collectors made of tarps.
*   **Implication:** Water hệ thống failed; desperation set in.
*   **Asset:** Makeshift graves in a playground.
*   **Implication:** High casualties; no space hoặc thời gian for proper burial.

### Layer 3: The Decay (Present)
*Shows nature reclaiming the world.*
*   **Asset:** Trees growing thông qua office floors.
*   **Implication:** It's been years, not days.
*   **Asset:** Rust streaks dripping down concrete.
*   **Implication:** Maintenance stopped long ago.
*   **Asset:** Animals nesting in vehicle engines.
*   **Implication:** Wildlife no longer fears machinery.

## "Micro-Stories" (Vignettes)

tạo small, self-contained scenes (dioramas) within the larger level.

### Example A: The Last Stand
*   **Setting:** A bathroom stall in a subway station.
*   **Props:** A skeleton clutching a revolver. One spent casing on the floor. Door barricaded from the inside.
*   **Story:** A survivor hid here, heard the monsters coming, realized escape was impossible, và took their own way out. NO TEXT LOG NEEDED.

### Example B: The Deal Gone Wrong
*   **Setting:** An alleyway behind a pharmacy.
*   **Props:** Two skeletons facing each other. A duffel bag of meds between them. Both have knives in their chests.
*   **Story:** A trade turned violent. Greed killed both parties. The loot (meds) is still there for the người chơi.

### Example C: The Hopeless Signal
*   **Setting:** A rooftop với a generator và radio tower.
*   **Props:** "SOS" painted in white on the roof. The generator is out of fuel. A skeleton sits by the radio, headphones on.
*   **Story:** Someone waited for rescue that never came.

## Loot Logic (Diegetic Placement)

Loot must make sense in its context. This rewards người chơi who *read* the environment.

*   **Medical Loot:** DO NOT put medkits in random wooden crates. Put them in:
    *   Ambulances
    *   Bathrooms (cabinets)
    *   Triage tents
    *   Offices (First Aid wall boxes)
*   **vũ khí Parts:** found on workbenches, gun store ruins, police trunks.
*   **Food:** Kitchens, supermarkets, vending machines (broken).

## Lighting as Narrative

cách dùng lighting to highlight these stories, nhưng also to set the cảm xúc tone.
*   **Red Light:** Danger, emergency, "Do Not Enter". (Naturally attracts người chơi due to curiosity).
*   **Green Light:** Safety, medical, "Exit".
*   **Flickering Light:** Instability, recent damage, tension. (e.g., A light flickering near a vent suggests something is moving inside the vent).

---

## Narrative Anti-Patterns

1.  **Written ghi chú everywhere:** Avoid "My Dearest Diary..." trang scattered in illogical places. cách dùng environmental clues first.
2.  **Blood mà không a Body:** nếu there is a massive blood trail, it must lead *somewhere* (a body, a drag mark, a monster den). It cannot just end at a wall.
3.  **Perfect Skeletons:** Skeletons nên được disarticulated, scavenged by animals, hoặc crushed. Avoid the "Halloween Prop" look.
