### Pre-Collapse History

#### District 14 at Its Peak (2020–2031)

District 14 was the commercial and cultural heart of the NAIC — the place where 200,000 workers came to live, shop, eat, and breathe. While Sector 7 was the industrial engine, District 14 was its soul.

| Location             | Pre-Collapse Function                                                     | Population/Traffic        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| **Central Plaza**    | Public square + transit hub. Rallying point for protests and celebrations | 30,000+ daily transit     |
| **Shopping Mall**    | 4-floor luxury mall — brands, food court, cinema, electronics             | 20,000 daily shoppers     |
| **Apartment Blocks** | Residential towers A–E — housing for 50,000 NAIC workers                  | 50,000 residents          |
| **Hotel Grand**      | 5-star hotel, business conference center, rooftop restaurant              | Visiting executives       |
| **Subway System**    | 3-line metro connecting all NAIC districts                                | 100,000 daily riders      |
| **City Park**        | Green space — the only natural respite in the dense urban grid            | Families, morning joggers |

#### The Corporate-Residential Divide

District 14 housed all classes of NAIC society in uncomfortably close proximity:

```
Hotel Grand (Floor 1–20): Nexus Corp executives and guest suites
                ↕ (same block)
Apartment Tower E (Floor 1–30): Factory workers, cleaners, security staff

The elevator from the Hotel's executive floors ran to the same lobby
as the bus stop that took workers to Sector 7 at 5AM.

Nexus internal memo (2029): "Residential proximity to executive amenities
is causing friction. Workers can see the rooftop restaurant from their
apartments. Consider relocating facility."

[Handwritten note in margin: "Do NOT relocate. Worker proximity to
executive spaces appears to suppress unionization activity."]
```

### The Collapse: What Happened in District 14

#### Week-by-Week Timeline (January–March 2031)

| Timeframe               | Event                                                                                      | Key Location                        | In-Game Evidence                                                         |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Week 1 (Jan 15–22)**  | Power blackout. Initial panic and looting                                                  | Central Plaza, Mall                 | Smashed ATMs, looted store windows, overturned market stalls             |
| **Week 2 (Jan 22–29)**  | Crimson Flu hits the dense residential population hard                                     | Apartments, improvised clinics      | Medical triage notes in apartment lobbies; makeshift quarantine barriers |
| **Week 3 (Feb 1–8)**    | Military quarantine declared — District 14 sealed                                          | City Park perimeter                 | Military barricades at park edges; "QUARANTINE ZONE" signs still posted  |
| **Week 4 (Feb 8–15)**   | Hospital overruns; partial evacuation via subway begins                                    | Hospital (off-map) + Subway Station | Abandoned medical equipment in subway; evacuation lists posted on walls  |
| **Month 2 (Mar)**       | Scavengers arrive. First survivor communities form                                         | Apartment Blocks A–C                | Early barricades on apartment doors; first "community rules" boards      |
| **Month 3+ (Apr)**      | Underground Network arrives; uses subway tunnels as HQ                                     | Subway System                       | Underground graffiti, dead drops, coded frequency etchings               |
| **2033–2036 (Present)** | Peacekeepers establish Hotel as command post; civilian communities stabilize in apartments | Hotel, Apartments, Park             | Peacekeeper signage, Dr. Wells' medical tent in Hotel lobby              |

#### "The Last Doctor" — A Narrative Thread

One of District 14's most haunting stories is delivered in pieces across multiple locations:

**Audio log fragments from Dr. Fatima Osei, Emergency Physician:**

> _Day 1 — Hotel ballroom (improvised triage): "We have 200 patients and supplies for 40. I'm making decisions no doctor should have to make. The ones I send away — I tell myself they might find help elsewhere. I know they won't."_

> _Day 14 — Apartment Block B lobby: "The quarantine team left yesterday. Just... left. Commander Vance came personally to tell me military medical support was being 'reassigned.' She looked me in the eyes. She knew what she was doing."_

> _Day 45 — Hotel rooftop garden, portable recorder: "Day 45. I'm still here. Twelve patients are still here. We've figured out what keeps them stable — it's not what the protocols said. I think I'm learning something. If anyone finds this, find the notebook in Room 714. It matters."_

**Room 714 (Hotel, Floor 7):** Contains Dr. Osei's medical notebook — a quest item that advances the Crimson Flu cure plotline.

### Present Day (2036): What Players Find

#### Overall Atmosphere

District 14 five years later is a study in contrasts — nature reclaiming glass and concrete while human communities persist in the ruins.

```
ATMOSPHERIC MAP — DISTRICT 14

SHOPPING MALL (Core Hot Zone)
  Sound:  Fallen glass crunching + distant squatter voices + skylights creating wind channels
  Light:  Skylight shafts through broken ceiling + emergency exit signs (battery still holds)
  Mood:   RESOURCE TENSION — treasure surrounded by danger; everyone wants what's here

CENTRAL PLAZA (Contested)
  Sound:  Wind across open concrete + radio static from hanging speakers (looping old broadcast)
  Light:  Open sky — best visibility on the map; horrible for cover
  Mood:   HIGH ALERT — no place to hide; engagement is unavoidable

SUBWAY SYSTEM (Underground Network territory)
  Sound:  Industrial fans + distant train bell still ringing automatically + water dripping
  Light:  Near-darkness; bioluminescent Underground markings glow faintly
  Mood:   CLAUSTROPHOBIC PURPOSE — someone else's domain; you're a guest

HOTEL GRAND (Peacekeeper territory)
  Sound:  Peacekeeper radio chatter + Dr. Wells' medical monitors beeping
  Light:  Emergency lighting + one floor with actual power (Peacekeepers rigged a generator)
  Mood:   FRAGILE ORDER — the only place with rules; feels both safe and desperate

APARTMENT BUILDINGS (Civilian Communities)
  Sound:  Children's voices (distant), cooking smells escaping through windows, daily community sounds
  Light:  Handmade lanterns + actual civilian-maintained generator on one block
  Mood:   BITTERSWEET HUMANITY — real life continuing; players should feel the weight of disturbing it

CITY PARK (Peacekeeper patrols)
  Sound:  Wind + birds nesting in old trees + patrol footsteps
  Light:  Natural daylight (park is relatively open)
  Mood:   FALSE SAFETY — Park extraction points make this active; never truly safe
```

### POI Profiles

#### 🔴 Shopping Mall — "The Survival Market"

**Zone Type:** A-Tier Hot Zone\
**Tactical Overview:**

* 2 floors + mezzanine level
* Skylights create dramatic lighting and exposure; broken skylights open rooftop sniping angles
* Food court is the primary ambush point (open, centrally located)
* Underground routes connect from the Mall Basement to Subway Station (NPC knowledge only — quest-gated)

**Lore Background:**

The Mall became District 14's first major crisis point during Week 1 — it was the closest source of food and medicine when the police abandoned the area. What followed was 72 hours of chaos that shaped every survivor community that came after: some people shared, some people hoarded, some people killed for a box of canned goods.

**Environmental Traces:**

| Location          | Trace                                                                                         | Implication                                             |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Main entrance     | Smashed automatic doors — pushed outward                                                      | People escaped this way during the surge, not in        |
| Food court        | Two tables set for 4 people — meals half-eaten, chairs pushed back in haste                   | The first wave of panic hit mid-meal                    |
| Electronics store | Empty shelves — not from looting; inventory sheets show organized removal                     | Someone systematically stripped this store before chaos |
| Cinema lobby      | Improvised fortification — ticket booth as barricade, concession area organized as food store | Survivors held the cinema for weeks post-Collapse       |
| Rooftop parking   | 3 cars carefully arranged as windbreak — sleeping bags inside                                 | Someone lived here through the first winter             |

**Collectible Document — "Mall Management Memo":**

> _From: District 14 Mall Director | To: Security Team | Date: January 16, 2031, 9:47 AM_ _Priority: URGENT. All security personnel are to prevent access to Basement Storage until Nexus Corp Regional Management authorizes distribution. The food stores in Basement Level 2 are Nexus property and are NOT to be distributed to the public regardless of circumstances. This is a legal matter. Compliance is mandatory._
>
> _\[Handwritten at bottom, different pen]: The mall director left on the Nexus executive helicopter at 11:00 AM. We distributed the food. — Security Chief Park Joon-ho_

#### 🔴 Central Plaza — "The Open Wound"

**Zone Type:** S-Tier Contested Zone\
**Tactical Overview:**

* Entirely open square — zero natural cover except central fountain and market stalls
* Supply drop priority landing zone (announced when active)
* Underground subway grates at four corners provide covert entrances/exits
* Multiple elevated overwatch positions from surrounding buildings

**Lore Background:**

The Plaza was District 14's beating heart — celebrations, protests, weddings, and vigils all happened here. In the first week of The Collapse, it became the site of the largest gathering (and the largest incident of civil violence) in NAIC history.

**Environmental Traces:**

* Protest banners still attached to lamp posts: "NEXUS = MURDER" (from pre-Collapse Break Nexus protests)
* Memorial "wall" constructed after The Collapse — thousands of photos and notes attached to a chain-link fence section. Now weathered, some still legible
* The central fountain was the site of organized water distribution — ladle marks worn into the stone edge from being used as a water scoop
* Radio tower on the adjacent building: still broadcasting the automated Nexus Corp welcome message ("Welcome to the Neo-Asia Industrial Corridor — Your Future Starts Here.") on repeat. Players can hear it faintly throughout the zone.

***

#### 🟣 Subway System — "The Underground's Kingdom"

**Zone Type:** B-Tier Mid Zone — Underground Network (Primary)\
**Three Subway Stations with Tunnel Network Connecting Them:**

**Tactical Core:**

* Near-darkness — requires flashlight or NVG in late match (post-dusk)
* Bioluminescent Underground Network markings provide minimal navigation light (if player has rep 2+, they can read the map system)
* Grenade acoustics dramatically amplified in tunnels
* Fastest cross-map travel route (if you survive the Underground patrols)

**Lore Background:**

The subway was District 14's circulatory system before The Collapse. When the quarantine sealed the district, the subway became the only way in or out. The Underground Network recognized this immediately — within weeks of The Collapse, they controlled every tunnel junction. They charge for passage; they provide information; they run the black market that keeps the apartment communities supplied.

**Audio Logs:**

```
DIS14-SUB-001: "The Network's Market"
Speaker: "Rat King" (Underground Network — primary field operative)
Location: Central Subway Station — spray-painted frequency indicator on wall
Trigger Object: Radio frequency receiver (requires Underground quest item to tune)

"If you're hearing this on the correct frequency — welcome.
 If you found this by accident — you're either very lucky or
 very good at finding things. Either way, we can do business.
 The tunnels are open to traders. The tunnels are not open
 to Peacekeepers. Or their informants. The three things we
 sell: information, passage, and the knowledge that some
 walls have doors. Ask about W2. Ask about Room 714.
 Leave something useful in the supply box near the turnstile.
 Fair trades keep everyone alive. Rat King out."

Lore: Confirms Underground's commercial model; references W2 (Sector 7 connection) and Room 714 (Hotel quest item)
Quest Connection: Triggers "The Network's Terms" questline
```

#### 🛡️ Hotel Grand — "The Peacekeeper Outpost"

**Zone Type:** B-Tier Mid Zone — Peacekeepers (Primary)\
**Tactical Overview:**

* 7 floors + rooftop bar/garden
* Peacekeepers maintain genuine control here — armed NPCs patrol all floors
* Dr. Wells' medical station is in the lobby (healing station for players with Peacekeeper rep 2+)
* Rooftop extraction (EX-D) requires flare gun but provides best elevated view

**Lore Background:**

Commander Vance chose the Hotel Grand for the Peacekeeper outpost because it was the most defensible structure near the civilian communities. Five years on, the Hotel is the closest thing to functional governance in District 14. It's not comfortable or safe, but it has rules. Rules that actually work most of the time.

**Environmental Traces:**

| Floor             | Trace                                                                                              | Meaning                                                                    |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lobby             | Dr. Wells' medical station — meticulous, labeled, well-supplied                                    | Active organization; someone is working hard here                          |
| Floor 1           | Guest register — last Nexus Corp entries dated Jan 14, 2031. Current Peacekeeper duty roster below | The hotel was in use until the last moment                                 |
| Floor 3–6         | Peacekeeper patrol schedule posted                                                                 | Active governance — this is a working HQ                                   |
| Floor 7, Room 714 | Dr. Osei's notebook — pages filled with medical observations during The Collapse                   | Quest item — piece of the Crimson Flu cure chain                           |
| Rooftop           | Peacekeeper radio tower + the ruins of the rooftop restaurant                                      | A functioning communications hub next to fine dining abandoned mid-service |

***

#### ⚪ Apartment Blocks A–E — "Where People Still Live"

**Zone Type:** C-Tier Mid Zone — Civilian Communities (Neutral)\
**Unique Property:** Live NPCs — non-hostile civilian characters

**Lore Background:**

Five years after The Collapse, 3,000–5,000 civilians still live in the District 14 apartment blocks. Not because they couldn't leave — because they chose to build something here. Community councils, shared food stores, medical rotations, education for children. Fragile, imperfect, real.

**Design Note:** These are the only map zones with living civilian NPCs who are explicitly non-hostile. Players who harm civilians receive a -500 reputation penalty with ALL factions simultaneously (non-negotiable design rule — this reinforces the game's moral framework).

**Environmental Traces:**

* Community announcements board in each building lobby (rotating handwritten updates: "Water supply restored Block A — thanks to Mei's repair work")
* Children's artwork on corridor walls — crayoned drawings of the NAIC before The Collapse, idealized and cheerful
* Functional community garden on Block C roof — actual food growing
* Careful organization: this is NOT the chaos of the rest of the map. That contrast is intentional and should be visually obvious.
