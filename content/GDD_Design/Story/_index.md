## 📖 The World of Aethelgard

A post-collapse world where corporations, scavengers, and anomalies fight for the remnants of the old world. The story is not told through cutscenes, but through the **environment** and **flavor text**.

### Narrative Delivery
1.  **Implicit:** A skeleton holding a picture of his family.
2.  **Explicit:** Quest descriptions and terminal logs.
3.  **Emergent:** The story players create themselves through gameplay.
